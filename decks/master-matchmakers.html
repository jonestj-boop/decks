The gap: Campaigns actually bid toward the recorded 1.28 conversions. The native site tag, tracking the same window, recorded 37 completions — roughly 29 times more.

Why the gap exists

The Primary goal is imported from GA4 under data-driven attribution across paid and organic channels. A single lead's credit gets split across every channel that touched them, not counted whole against the click that closed it. That is why every conversion figure in the account's export is a decimal rather than a whole number.

Two more likely contributors

  • Cross-domain tracking between the marketing site and the client portal may not be fully configured, which can break the session on that handoff.
  • The trigger fires on page load, not form-submission success, so a slow load or a blocker can drop a real lead silently.
Fix first: Smart Bidding has been optimizing against a fraction of real demand for the entire flight. This gets corrected before architecture or bidding strategy — the next slide is exactly how.

Fix #1 · Month 1 Priority

How we fix the tracking — week one, before anything else moves.

Four defined fixes, in dependency order — no site rebuild required, all inside the first 30 days.

# The fix What actually changes When
1 Rebuild the primary conversion action Replace the GA4-imported goal with a native Google Ads conversion that fires on form-submission success, not page load. Demote the GA4 imports to Secondary so they still report but stop driving bids. Week 1
2 Repair cross-domain tracking Configure the linker between the marketing site and the client portal so the session survives the handoff instead of dropping the click that earned the lead. Week 1–2

  • Conversions report as whole numbers, not decimals.
  • Volume lands near the 37 the native tag recorded, not 1.28.
  • A live test click traces cleanly through to a recorded conversion.

Fix #1 · Implementation

What the rebuilt lead path actually looks like.

A hosted form and one new thank-you page — no CMS development required to instrument any of it.

1

Ad click

Google appends the click ID to your landing page URL.

2

Landing page

A small script reads the click ID and campaign tags off the URL and passes them into the form.

3

Hosted form

Three short steps instead of nine fields, photo upload last. Hidden fields carry the click ID through.

4

Thank-you page

On your domain, reachable only after a real submission. This is where the conversion fires.

5

CRM → Google Ads

Click ID stored on the contact. Leads that become clients get uploaded back as conversions.

Why a hosted form

  • Your site runs on DNN — a hosted form means no development cycle every time measurement needs a change.
  • Multi-step with the upload last, and partial-submission data showing exactly where people quit.
  • Runs on your own subdomain with your branding, so the funnel still looks like Master Matchmakers.

The part that changes bidding

  • At best, the account today could optimize toward form fills.
  • With the click ID stored, we upload the leads that became consultations and clients.
  • Smart Bidding then chases revenue, not raw submissions — the right objective for a high-consideration service.
Why it can't break the same way twice: the conversion fires on a page that is only reachable after a genuine submission, and it fires before the client-portal handoff. Both failure modes from the previous slide are designed out rather than patched over.

Creative & Landing Pages

The landing pages are strong. The ads pointing to them are not.

Your own site gives paid media more to work with than the ads currently use.

What's working

  • Dedicated, well-built pages exist for nearly every city and state in the campaign list.
  • Local testimonials on the geo pages, plus a comparison table against dating apps.
  • Real trust stats: 25 years in business, 6,000+ clients matched, a 4.6 ★ rating, and BBB A+ accreditation.

The gaps

  • Ad strength sits at average to poor across Performance Max and Search.
  • Missing sitelinks and callout extensions, with mediocre RSA headlines.
  • Performance Max's final URL drifted from a focused landing microsite to the full homepage.
  • Demand Gen sends cold traffic straight into a 9-field form that requires a photo upload.
Fastest win available: None of this requires new content. Sitelinks and callouts can be built directly from material the brand has already published — this is a same-week fix.
