The Next Frontier

From cited to recommended

You're already being cited in AI Overviews for "best matchmaker in Beverly Hills" — Google reads your posts. The goal now is being named in the answer itself. Here's the difference — and how we close it.

🔗

Cited — where you are

  • Your own posts are a source Google pulls facts from
  • You appear as a reference link under the overview
  • You inform the answer — but you're not the pick
⭐

Recommended — where we're going

  • The AI names Master Matchmakers inside the answer
  • Driven by independent sources vouching for you
  • You become the shortlist, not the footnote

How we close the gap

💬

1 · Comment velocity

More Reddit mentions that recommend you by name for the exact city. Repetition across independent threads is what the model reads as consensus. The fastest lever.

🤝

2 · Third-party consensus

Land in the "best matchmaker in [city]" roundups and review platforms — Google, Yelp, BBB — that Overviews quote. Independent corroboration is what turns a citation into a recommendation.

🏛️

3 · Entity authority

Schema, Google Business Profile, consistent listings & Wikidata — so the model firmly links "Master Matchmakers" to the category + city it's answering about.

Rule of thumb: self-published content gets you cited; independent sources naming you get you recommended. Reddit velocity is the quickest way to build that consensus — exactly what scaling up your Reddit tier delivers.

Own Your Name

Owning the "Master Matchmakers reviews" overview

Being recommended for the category is one job. Controlling the AI Overview for your own brand is another — and today that result pulls from a fragmented, sometimes off-brand source pool. Here's what's feeding it, and how we take it over.

What's feeding the overview today

  • — Five conflicting ratings across platforms — 4.4 to 4.6 stars, plus a 3.69 on BBB. Mixed signals for Google to reconcile.
  • — Off-brand & competitor pages rank for your name — Forbes "best matchmaking" lists, third-party review sites, even "The Matchmaking Company" (a different business).
  • — BBB complaints surface in the same pool.
  • — No single owned source dominates — so the overview grabs whatever's loosely relevant, including threads & videos that aren't really about you.

How we take it over

  • — Consolidate reviews — Google Business Profile first, then unify Yelp / BBB / Trustpilot with volume & recency behind one consistent rating.
  • — Lock the entity — Organization schema with sameAs links, Wikidata, and a Knowledge-Panel claim, so Google is certain which "Master Matchmakers" you are.
  • — Own the source pool — a branded reviews page with Review schema, plus on-brand Reddit & YouTube that are about you.
  • — Displace the wrong sources — out-rank the off-brand citations with stronger branded assets.
Set the expectation: entity and review signals reshape an AI Overview over weeks, not days — but once you own the source pool, the result is durable and hard for anyone else to hijack.
