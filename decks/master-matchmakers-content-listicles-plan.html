The $4,500 model
Build the base, then launch the listicles

Two placements a month is too thin to register in the category, and five placements plus a full content month run past $4,500 together. So we build first and launch last: content at the full 60 a month with Reddit seeding your markets through October and November, then all five listicle placements in December. No month runs over $4,500, and nothing publishes fast enough to trip Google's mass-publishing flags.

Why place the five last: a best-of article that names you the top pick lands harder when the site behind it already carries the content and the Reddit presence to back the claim. So October and November build that base at the full 60 articles a month, then December hits the category with five placements at once, the volume that actually registers. The one trade is December's content, which sits at 32 to fund the launch, so the quarter lands near 152 of the 180 planned articles.
