Partner Program Pricing

Two ways to work with EmberTribe — tiered so that bringing more clients earns a better rate.

How it works: Choose the model that fits how you want to work with us. Bring us in as a named delivery partner while you stay the face of the account, or hand us the client relationship and earn recurring referral revenue instead. Reach out to your EmberTribe contact to get started or ask questions about either path.
1

Two Engagement Models

Every partner picks one of two structures. Both draw pricing from the same core service menu (AI Visibility, Local SEO, SEO retainers, Paid Media, and more). What differs is who owns the client relationship and how EmberTribe gets paid for volume.

Model A

EmberTribe as Partner Agency

  • Partner's choice: introduce EmberTribe to the client as a named delivery partner, or keep EmberTribe fully behind the scenes and handle all communication themselves
  • Partner agency remains the client's main point of contact and holds the client relationship either way
  • If introduced, EmberTribe joins client calls as needed (1/mo base, more available) and answers client questions via email/Slack
  • If not introduced, EmberTribe never contacts the client — all syncs and support happen with the partner instead
  • Contract is between EmberTribe and the partner agency
  • Partner buys at a discounted partner rate and sets their own client-facing price
Model B

EmberTribe as Client Owner

  • EmberTribe is in contract directly with the client
  • EmberTribe is the main point of contact
  • Full standard support and delivery — same as any direct client
  • Partner's role ends at the referral (no fulfillment responsibility)
  • Partner is paid a recurring revenue share

Model A — Partner Agency Tiers

EmberTribe is brought in as a partner agency working under the partner's client relationship. The partner remains the primary point of contact and contract holder while EmberTribe delivers the work, and separately chooses whether EmberTribe is introduced to the client or stays fully behind the scenes (see below). Discount is applied off EmberTribe's standard rate card. Tier is set by the partner's total active client accounts under EmberTribe management, reviewed quarterly on a trailing basis.

Non-Compete & Client Protection (Model A)

Because the partner is introducing EmberTribe to their own client under Model A, EmberTribe protects that relationship with a non-compete / non-circumvention commitment covering every client account introduced under Model A.

Protection Terms

ElementTerm
CoverageAny client account EmberTribe was introduced to, or serviced, through the partner under Model A — not an agency-wide non-compete
RestrictionEmberTribe will not directly solicit, contract with, or pitch competing services to a covered client without the partner's written consent
Active-term protectionFull protection for the entire duration of the partner agreement
Post-termination tail12 months
Extended tail — Gold/Platinum partners24 months
Why this term length
12 months is the standard baseline for agency-to-agency non-circumvention protection — long enough to remove any incentive to wait out the partnership, short enough to stay reasonable for everyone. Gold and Platinum partners get 24 months as recognition for the volume they've brought.

Carve-Outs

  • Client-initiated, unsolicited inbound contact is not a violation — but the partner is notified and given right of first refusal before EmberTribe engages
  • Protection can be voided if the partner is terminated for cause (e.g., non-payment, material breach)
  • Continued fulfillment work delivered through the partner relationship itself is never a violation — the clause only restricts going around the partner, not working through them

Model B — Referral Revenue-Share Tiers

No discount here — EmberTribe carries full delivery and support cost as the client-facing team, so unit pricing stays at rate card. Instead, the partner earns a recurring share of monthly revenue collected from each client they referred. Standard practice is to pay out for 6 months or the client's initial contract term, whichever is longer — payout duration then extends by tier as a reward for volume, alongside the rev-share percentage. Tier is set by total active referred clients, reviewed quarterly on a trailing basis.

Shared Rules

  • Underlying service pricing is fixed — both models discount or share revenue off EmberTribe's standard rate card. They do not renegotiate unit pricing or scope per service.
  • Tier review cadence: quarterly, based on trailing active account/referral count. A partner who drops below a tier's threshold for two consecutive quarters steps down at the next renewal.
  • Minimum engagement: each underlying client engagement follows EmberTribe's standard engagement rules (3-month minimum commitment by default).
  • Ad spend: always billed at cost in both models — never discounted (Model A) and never counted toward the revenue-share base (Model B).
  • No mixing models per client: a given end client is either serviced through a partner agency (Model A) or directly contracted with EmberTribe on referral (Model B) — not both.
  • Beyond Tier 4: volume above 15 accounts/referrals in either model is a custom conversation with your EmberTribe partnerships contact, not an auto-applied rate.
