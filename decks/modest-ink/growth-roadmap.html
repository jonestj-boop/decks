What we deliberately excluded

  • Services you don't offer: teeth whitening, threading, scalp micropigmentation, and paramedical tattoo searches that rode along in the data. No content that promises what you don't do.
  • Cosmetics-product searches: "lip stain" and "brow powder" queries are makeup shoppers, not PMU clients. High volume, wrong buyer, cut on purpose.
  • Competitor-brand searches, including rival studios and academies. Ranking on someone's brand name converts poorly and reads as hostile.
  • Out-of-market cities: NYC, Houston, and other-state searches that inflate numbers without ever booking a Murray appointment.
