What this means for your practice

About 1,100 of your pages are cited as sources. Your name appears 26 times. AI engines are reading your writing to answer questions about PCOS, endometriosis and menopause, and almost never telling anyone where the answer came from. You are the source; someone else is the recommendation.
ChatGPT is the one that matters most for you. It is where people now ask for a doctor by name, and it cites 23 of your pages while naming you three times. When a woman in Los Angeles asks ChatGPT for an endometriosis surgeon, your practice is not in the answer even though your pages helped write it.
This is your search problem in a newer place. Being cited without being named is the AI equivalent of ranking on page one and taking no clicks. The fix is the same: pages that state plainly who you are, where you practise, and what you treat.

Your deepest surgical territory and the one with the most unserved commercial demand. Covers the decision between removing fibroids and removing the uterus, recovery in real detail, and the questions women ask before booking.

You already have nineteen menopause pages and they are the strongest genuinely on-brand block on your site. This pillar extends them into hormone therapy decisions rather than symptom lists.

The bottom of the funnel. Written for a woman in your city deciding who to see, covering how to judge surgical training, what to ask, and what your practice does differently.

Excision versus ablation, why diagnosis takes years, and what surgery can and cannot resolve. Held at sixteen rather than more because your existing pages already rank for much of this cluster.

PCOS · 16 articles

Held at sixteen on purpose. Your PCOS pages already rank for most of this territory, so the bigger opportunity is rewriting them rather than competing with them.

What we deliberately excluded

  • 27,322 general health searches. Period timing, itchy skin, fetal hiccups and similar. This is the traffic you already have, it is the reason your click count looks healthy, and it produces no patients.
  • 596 searches outside what you sell. Supplement and self-dosing questions, weight-loss drug comparisons, and obstetrics. Obstetrics was excluded at your direction, whatever the volume. General fertility and IVF went with it; fibroid surgery that preserves fertility stayed, because that is something you do.
  • Procedures you do not perform. Uterine fibroid embolization and focused ultrasound have real search demand. Targeting them would bring you women looking for a procedure you do not offer.
  • Searches outside Los Angeles. You rank for PCOS specialist searches across eight Florida towns. They produced 10,219 impressions and zero clicks in ninety days, and cannot produce a patient.
  • 159 searches your own pages already surface for. Writing new articles against your own ranking pages is the most expensive mistake available here. These became a refresh list instead, and they are in the workbook.
  • 12 articles we talked ourselves out of. They target searches a page of yours already covers, so they are marked as refreshes of that page instead of new URLs. Publishing a second page against your own is the most expensive mistake available here.
