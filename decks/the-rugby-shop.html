How AI tools choose

AI assistants recommend the shops the rest of the web talks about.

ChatGPT, Google's AI Overviews, and Perplexity don't browse your catalog. They summarize what they find about you on your site, in reviews, on Reddit, and in roundup articles. Each tool leans on different sources.

What that means for The Rugby Shop

  • Your site has to state the facts clearly: who you are, what you sell, where you ship, and why you're credible
  • The same facts need to appear on partner sites, directories, and rugby communities, worded the same way
  • Rugby players already swap gear advice on Reddit and forums. Those threads get quoted in AI answers
  • Fresh, useful articles give AI tools something to cite. A blog last updated in 2022 gives them nothing
Week one baseline: we'll test 30 real buyer prompts across all four tools and record who gets named today, so every later report compares against a fixed starting point.
