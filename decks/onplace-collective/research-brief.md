# OnPlace Collective: pitch research brief

EmberTribe pitch for SEO (new website), ClusterMagic, and organic LinkedIn for Monaca Smith Onstad (CEO). Their main goal is business development. Research pulled Nov 13, 2026. Raw crawl data is in `data/`.

## Who they are (from their own site)
- OnPlace Collective, Sarasota FL + new Houston office ("Houston Office Now Open!" on the homepage).
- Positioning: "the trusted leader in Social Architecture™." They turn "parcels and plats" into communities developers can sell.
- Three companies:
  - **OnMenity**: amenity concepting and planning with project teams.
  - **OnSpire**: marketing for communities (budgets, branded experiences, sales performance). They are a marketing shop themselves, so the pitch has to respect that.
  - **OnVie**: lifestyle programming and activation.
- Buyer: developers and builders of master-planned communities (MPCs).

## SEO baseline: current onplace.life
WordPress, default WP sitemaps, no SEO plugin.
- **11 pages total. No blog.** Sitemap: home, Why OPC, OnMenity, OnSpire, OnVie, About, Contact, Careers, Smith Lane, Episodes, Accessibility.
- **0 of 11 pages have a meta description.** No Open Graph tags on any page. No schema anywhere (no Organization, LocalBusiness, PodcastSeries, or Person).
- Homepage title is just "OnPlace." Homepage H1s are "onmenity / onspire / onvie" repeated twice. 6 of 11 pages have no H1 at all. The Smith Lane page has 5 H1s.
- Brand pages are thin: OnSpire 154 words, OnVie 166, OnMenity 244.
- About 90% of images have no alt text (e.g. 101 of 103 on the episodes page).
- **Podcast SEO is broken.** All episode show notes live on one 11,700-word page. Each episode uses a `?episode=48` URL that canonicals back to `/smith-lane-episodes/`, so Google treats ~49 episodes as one page. Separate `/podcast/` pages exist for 38 older episodes but are 61 words each. Season 3 has no individual pages in the sitemap.
- Pages are fast (0.2 to 0.5s server response, 14 to 44 KB HTML). Speed is not the problem.
- **Migration risk:** 38 `/podcast/` URLs, 11 pages, 25 tag archives. All need redirect mapping before the new site launches.
