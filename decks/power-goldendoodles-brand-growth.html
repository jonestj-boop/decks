If the team only starts five things

The quick-win five.

Highest return for the least lift, and all five are things the kids can own with light oversight. Get these running first, then work down the full list.

01

Automate the review ask

A scheduled email or text ~1–2 weeks after each puppy goes home, with a one-tap Google review link. Your reviews are the single biggest asset, so make asking automatic.

02

Wake up YouTube

Retitle and re-tag the videos you already have, sort them into playlists, and cut short clips. Years of footage is sitting there doing almost nothing.

03

Start the waitlist nurture

A simple email sequence for people not ready yet: litter updates, tips, owner stories. They watch for a year, so stay in the inbox.

04

Open a Pinterest

Puppy and breed content lives for years there and sends steady traffic. A natural fit nobody in the niche is working.

05

Launch an owner community

A private group for Power Goldendoodle families. It runs on its own once started and becomes your engine for referrals, reviews, and content.
