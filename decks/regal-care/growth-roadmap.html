The local map pack

The 3-pack is a second race — and the one that fills beds

Above the blue links, Google pins three businesses on a map. For a board & care home that 3-pack is the highest-intent surface in local search: a family in Camarillo pulls out a phone, types “assisted living near me,” and picks from three. Ranking there is a different game from the organic one on slide 2 — and a faster one to win.

FactorWhat it meansYou today

Two of the three are things we build. You can’t move the houses, but relevance and prominence are exactly what this plan installs — and locally, they are the two levers the directories can’t buy their way past.
