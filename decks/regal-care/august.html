The Paradox

More visibility, fewer clicks — the exact opposite of what should happen.

The roadmap's baseline was your first 6 days on record in Search Console (Jul 13–18): 1,691 impressions, 51 clicks. Here's that same 6-day window compared to the most recent 6 days of live data (Jul 29–Aug 3).

Avg. position here is an impression-weighted GSC calculation for this specific 6-day window — a different metric from the roadmap's separately-sourced 10.3 average (a SEMrush figure across all 141 tracked keywords), not a contradiction of it.

Impressions rose 19.5% while clicks fell 39.2%. This isn't a rankings collapse — average position is roughly flat. Something between "Google shows the result" and "a family clicks it" got worse.

What this rules in and out

It isn't a demand problem. More people are searching and seeing Regal Care in results than at baseline — impressions are up across the board, not down.
It's a click-through problem. CTR fell from 3.0% to 1.5% — roughly half as many of the people who now see you are choosing to click. Worth checking: has an AI Overview, local map pack, or competitor snippet started absorbing the click on your target queries?
The Article Plan and roadmap remain unactioned. The 120-article plan built to establish Regal Care as the clear answer for these searches hasn't gone live yet — every week without it is a week competitors have to close the gap.

What this means

Citations spread from 1 engine to 3. In July, only ChatGPT cited regalcare.org directly (1 page). Now ChatGPT, Google AI Overview, and Google AI Mode all cite it — Gemini is the one holdout, still citing zero Regal Care pages despite 16 mentions.
Google AI Overview earned both more mentions (2→5) and its first citation (0→1) — worth watching alongside the falling click-through on the previous slides. If AI Overview is surfacing Regal Care more, it may also be part of what's now absorbing the click before it reaches a traditional result.
The fix identified in the roadmap — schema markup and answer-shaped content — still hasn't shipped. These early gains are happening without it. A deliberate push would likely move the score itself, not just the citation count.
