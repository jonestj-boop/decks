Review velocity

You don't need 1,300 reviews. You need a steady weekly flow.

You took the shop from ~40 reviews to 292. The next step is making that a system, so fresh reviews land every week instead of in bursts.

The system

  • Every customer gets asked at pickup, then gets a text with the direct review link within 2 hours.
  • A reminder goes out at 3 days if they haven't left one.
  • Every review gets a reply within 48 hours that mentions the service and the neighborhood. Your replies count as content.
  • Rotate the ask to Yelp for 1 in 5 customers so Yelp stops lagging.
  • Front desk or techs earn a bonus for asks logged, tracked in the shop system.
On "isn't that illegal?" You were right to ask. The clean version: never pay or discount a customer for a review, ask every customer (not just the happy ones), and pay staff on asks made, never on star ratings. That keeps you inside Google's review policy and the FTC's 2024 fake-review rule.
