How buyers pick a dealer now

Two places decide who gets the visit.

A used-car shopper usually lands on a dealer from one of two screens. We work both, because the signals that win one also help the other.

Screen 01 · Google Maps

The map pack

The three dealers Google shows for "used car dealer near me" or "used Toyota Plano." Those three take most of the calls, direction requests and walk-ins. Rank 4 and below barely gets seen.

Screen 02 · AI assistants

The AI answer

ChatGPT, Google AI Mode and Perplexity answer "best used car dealer in Plano" with a short list. They build that list from review sites, local roundups and "best of" articles, so the dealers named there get named in the answer.

Part one

Guaranteed map pack.

Top three in Google Maps for an agreed keyword set, inside a set radius, within 90 days.

The guarantee

Top 3 for 10 to 15 keywords, 5 miles out, inside 90 days.

The radius is measured from the rooftop at 3333 W Plano Pkwy. Five miles covers most of Plano plus the edges of Carrollton, Richardson, Addison and south Frisco, which is where most of Gulliver's walk-in buyers live.

What's covered

  • 10 to 15 keywords agreed with the dealer before we start
  • Top 3 position in the Google Maps pack across the 5-mile radius
  • Rank checked from a grid of points across the radius, not one spot
  • A before scan on day one and the same scan every month

What's not

  • Organic (blue link) rankings
  • Rankings outside the agreed radius
Why 5 miles to start: it's the tightest ring around the lot, and it's where a guarantee is fastest to deliver. Dealers who want to pull buyers from McKinney, Frisco or Dallas can go to a 10-mile radius once the 5-mile ring is locked in.
One rooftop, one guarantee. Each dealer location gets its own radius and keyword set. A group with three rooftops gets three guarantees.

How we get there

A ranking engine, plus the work that makes rankings stick.

Plenty of tools can push a map listing up for a while. Rankings hold when Google also sees a busy profile and a dealer other sites talk about. We run all three.

Layer 01

Map ranking engine

Proximity and relevance signals worked across the full grid, aimed at the agreed keyword set. This is what carries the guarantee.

Layer 02

Profile that looks alive

Business profile tuned to the keyword set: categories, services, products, Q&A. Weekly posts and photos of new arrivals, plus listings matched across the major directories.

Layer 03

Brand mentions

Gulliver named on real, trafficked sites (part two). The same mentions that feed AI answers tell Google the dealer is a known local business.

What a brand mention is

Gulliver, named on a real site, for the question a buyer asks.

Each mention is a placement on an established site that already gets traffic: a "best of" roundup, a buying guide or a review piece. Gulliver is named in context next to the facts that make it worth recommending.

Every mention

  • Lives on a DR 30 to 60 site with 1,000+ monthly visitors
  • Is a listicle, review or guide matched to a real buyer question
  • Names Gulliver with real facts: rating, specialty, location
  • Is reported with the live URL every month
