Why this plan

Why this plan, and why 57 instead of 120

Four pillars, each tied to what you sell or to the groups your site names as buyers.

The pillars

World's Finest Chocolate Bars and Boxes · 9 pieces

Your product, explained: flavors, bar sizes, what happened to the $1 box, who makes it, and the homepage rewrite that answers where to buy it near Compton.

Chocolate Bar Fundraisers · 9 articles

How organizers run a chocolate sale, what they keep, consignment against paying per box, and where Sam's Club, Gordon Food Service and your counter fit.

Fundraisers for Sports Teams · 10 articles

Teams are one of the buyer groups your site names. One hub plus articles by sport and by group (youth leagues, boosters, marching band), each presenting a chocolate sale as one option among several.

School, Church and Club Fundraisers · 30 articles

Grade levels, PTAs, churches, youth groups and clubs. It is the largest pillar because this is where organizers start planning, weeks before they buy.

What we deliberately excluded

  • Chocolate for yourself, gifts, gourmet and products you don't sell. "Best chocolate in the world", "chocolate shop los angeles", pretzel fundraisers: 508 keywords, 195,550 searches
  • World's Finest's own brand and company searches. The bare brand name, jobs, headquarters, prize programs: 68 keywords, 63,770 searches
  • Other brands. See's, Hershey, Gertrude Hawk and brand lists: 227 keywords, 52,140 searches
  • Searches with no buying intent. A movie, contests, jobs, trivia: 113 keywords, 45,160 searches
  • Fundraising software, crowdfunding and homework. Donation platforms, GoFundMe-style searches, online selling and math word problems: 164 keywords, 39,900 searches
Raw research found 522,840 monthly searches. After cutting what you don't sell or can't serve, 114,850 remain, and this plan targets 107,080 of them. Going to 120 would have meant near-duplicates or topics that fit you poorly; the best of those wait on the Bench.

Each article is written from your verified facts only: the $48 box, what is inside each one, your hours, the walk-in model and the Spanish line. Where a chocolate sale fits an article, it appears as one option among the others, with directions to your counter.
