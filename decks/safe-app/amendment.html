Amendment No. 1 · November 2026

Term, flexible mix and termination

Amends the services agreement between Safe App and EmberTribe, LLC for paid media management, ClusterMagic content and brand mentions.

What this amendment does

This Amendment No. 1 ("Amendment") changes the services agreement between Safe App ("Client") and EmberTribe, LLC ("EmberTribe") issued through EmberTribe's proposal system at cns.embertribe.com (the "Agreement"). The sections listed below are replaced or added as written. Every other term of the Agreement stays the same. If this Amendment and the Agreement conflict, this Amendment controls. It takes effect on the Agreement's Effective Date, or on the date both parties sign it, whichever is later.

1. Purchase terms: Length (replaces the existing text)

Length: 6 months from the date this agreement is signed. It does not renew automatically, and any extension must be agreed in writing by both parties. There is no early buyout. Client may end the agreement at any time under Section 4(a).

2. Scope of Work (added under Pricing)

Flexible mix: The monthly fee is $10,000. The split between brand mentions, articles and paid media management may be adjusted month to month within that amount, based on performance, by email agreement of both parties.

Delivery schedule: Each month's brand mentions and articles are ordered in two equal halves, the first at the start of the billing period and the second at its midpoint.

Total: "Total over the term: $60,000" is replaced with "$60,000 if not terminated early."

3. Section 3, Term (replaces the whole section)

This Agreement is effective on the Effective Date and runs for the period stated in the Scope of Work (the "Term"). It does not renew automatically. The parties may extend it only by a written agreement signed by both.

4. Section 4, Termination (first paragraph unchanged; (a), (b) and (c) replaced)

(a) Client may terminate this Agreement at any time, including on the Effective Date, by giving fourteen (14) calendar days' written notice by email. The notice period begins on the date the email is sent (for example, notice sent November 25 ends the Agreement on December 9).

(b) On termination by Client, Client will pay (1) paid media management fees through the termination date, prorated daily for the final billing period, and (2) the full cost of any brand mentions and articles ordered before notice was sent, under the delivery schedule in the Scope of Work. EmberTribe will deliver that ordered work. Client owes no other fees for the rest of the Term.

(c) Any amount Client has prepaid beyond what is owed under 4(b) will be refunded within 15 days of the termination date.

The former Section 4(b) on performance pricing is deleted in full and does not apply to this Agreement.

5. Section 14(c), Notices (added at the end)

Notwithstanding the above, a termination notice from Client under Section 4(a) is effective on the date the email is sent.
