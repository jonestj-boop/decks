Why this plan

How we choose what to publish — and why we start narrow.

Every topic is scored on three things: real search volume, winnable difficulty, and fit with a member who'll actually post a bid request. We front-load the beachhead, then widen.

Criterion 01

Volume

Real, recurring demand in the trades your members transact — not vanity terms that never lead to a bid request.

Criterion 02

Difficulty

Winnable now given a brand-new domain. Long-tail and local first, head terms as authority builds — so wins come early.

Criterion 03

Fit

Searches on the path to joining or posting a project. Traffic that converts to members, not just pageviews.

Deliberately sequenced: we prove the content-to-member funnel in Minneapolis first, then clone the winning structure metro by metro. National head terms we can't win yet are parked — not chased — until the domain has earned the authority to take them.
