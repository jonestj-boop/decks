Why this plan

Why this plan, and what we said no to

The selection is the strategy. Here is the reasoning, including what we cut.

The pillars

Oahu Wedding Venues & Locations · 31 articles

Your venue hub already ranks for 31 keywords worth 12,310 searches a month, at positions 36 to 62. Twenty-two per-venue guides plus roundups feed authority into that hub, the same play that built Derek Wong's whole ranking footprint, executed deeper.

Weddings & Elopements · 15 articles

Hiring guides, destination-planning, engagement and proposal content around your highest-ticket service, pointed at /weddings and your elopement guide.

Family & Portrait Sessions · 6 articles

Hiring and session content for the family, senior, and resort-visitor market, supporting /family where you already rank at position 44.

Planning Your Hawaii Wedding · 5 articles

Coordinator, officiant, packages, and traditions content. You do not sell planning, but the couple who finds you while deciding these things hires photographers, and AI engines cite exactly this kind of page.

Photo Booth & Event Production · 5 articles

Booth, sound, and milestone-event content for your add-on services, including baby luau and debut coverage, where you have real services and the market has almost no content.

What we deliberately excluded

  • Competitor brand searches: 36 keywords, 4,290 vol. People searching 'derek wong' or 'right frame photography' want that studio. Content aimed at them reads as poaching and does not convert.
  • Maui, Kauai, and Big Island head terms. You serve neighbor islands with travel fees, but 'maui wedding photographer' belongs to on-island studios. Benched, not deleted; say the word and they come back.
  • DIY and props queries. 'Hawaiian photo booth props printable' is a Pinterest crafter, not a booth rental client.
  • Bare venue-name searches. 'Dillingham ranch' alone is someone looking for the ranch. We capture that demand through venue-guide articles instead of pretending to be the venue.
  • Maternity: benched pending one answer. Two of your ten biggest opportunities are maternity terms (2,020 searches a month combined). You have a maternity blog post but no service page. If you want to sell maternity, this becomes pillar six; that is a call question, not our call.
The honest math: raw research surfaced 505 keywords worth 78,860 monthly searches. After filtering out competitor brands, other-island head terms, DIY prop queries, and things you do not sell, the real pool is 270 keywords worth 47,030. We would rather hand you the smaller true number than the bigger useless one.
