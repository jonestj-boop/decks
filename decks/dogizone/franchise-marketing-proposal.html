The Short Version

This fits inside the economics you have already modeled

You raised a fair concern on our June call: that our franchise pricing looked higher than what competing brands charge. We have rebuilt the proposal to fit your numbers rather than ask you to change them.

The recurring fee we are proposing is the $250/month digital marketing fee already in your draft. It is not an additional charge. Structured the way we recommend, it counts toward the Local Advertising Expense rather than sitting on top of it, so a franchisee's total marketing cost does not move at all. Camp Bow Wow uses exactly this mechanism with their $300/month platform fee.

What changes for you: nothing. Your franchise fee, royalty, brand fund, local advertising requirement and digital marketing fee all stay as drafted. The only new item is a one-time launch package per location, paid from the franchisee's existing pre-opening marketing budget.

Scope and Pricing

Three lines, only one of which touches franchisee recurring cost

1. Per-location campaign management

$250 /location /month

Your existing digital marketing fee. Counts toward the Local Advertising Expense.

  • Google and Meta campaign management, geo-targeted to their location
  • Budget pacing and ongoing optimization
  • Monthly performance recap, delivered by email
  • Conversion tracking maintained and monitored
  • Escalation path when a franchisee needs support
  • Quarterly strategy review with DogiZone HQ

Deliberately not included, and this is what keeps the price here

  • Standing monthly calls with each franchisee. Reporting is written, on request escalation replaces the recurring call.
  • Custom creative per location. Assets are standardized and varied only by location name and geography.
  • Weekly reporting or journals. Monthly recap only.
  • Website work beyond the location page.
  • Ongoing local SEO beyond the launch setup.

2. Per-location launch package

$3,500 one-time

Paid at signing from the franchisee's pre-opening marketing budget.

  • Google Business Profile: creation, verification and full optimization
  • Categories, services, attributes, photos, Q&A and opening posts
  • Location page on the existing DogiZone domain, no new domains
  • Localized copy, keyword-targeted to that location's primary service
  • Campaign build: master campaign cloned, geo-targeted, copy localized
  • Tracking: conversion tracking plus a dedicated call tracking number
  • Review-collection setup and a franchisee playbook
  • Pre-opening campaign management through the launch window
  • Handoff: GBP keys to the franchisee plus one training session
For comparison: Hounds Town requires franchisees to purchase a $15,000 Pre-Opening Marketing Package from the franchisor. This is a fuller scope at less than a quarter of the price.

3. HQ program management

$1,500 /month

Paid by DogiZone HQ, not by franchisees. Moves to $2,500/month once five locations are open. Fundable from the brand fund once activated.

  • Master campaign architecture built and maintained
  • Creative and messaging templates kept current
  • Reporting infrastructure across the network
  • Brand governance and marketing standards enforcement
  • Onboarding readiness so a new location can launch on demand
  • Network-level performance reporting to you

How

Why we can price a location at $250 when we could not price a standalone business there

This only works because the franchise system is uniform. Everything below came out of our June conversation, and each one is a reason the cost per location stays low.

One ad account

Separate geo-targeted campaigns per location inside a single account, not separate accounts. Setup, structure and learnings are shared rather than rebuilt each time.

Cloned assets

Standardized creative customized only for location name and geography. No bespoke creative cycle per franchisee.

Written reporting

A monthly recap instead of standing calls. This is the single largest cost saving, and it is the relationship you said franchisees should have.

Compounding data

Every location feeds the same conversion dataset. Location ten launches with nine locations' worth of learning behind it.

Where our head start matters: we already run the Rockville location, know the Gingr funnel and its reporting quirks, have the conversion tracking and call tracking in place, and have completed the CRO audit on the site the location pages will live on. The master campaign gets built and proven on corporate, then cloned. Any other provider starts from zero on a brand with no location-level history.

Prerequisite

One thing that needs to happen before location one

Franchise location pages can only rank if the domain they sit on is structured to support them. Right now the site is not, and this is foundation work on the main DogiZone site rather than anything a franchisee pays for.

It covers the page architecture that lets location pages rank without cannibalizing each other, primary keyword mapping per service, and the Google Business Profile and review framework each new location inherits. Done once, every location benefits.

We would scope this as a fixed-scope, fixed-price project in the same shape as the CRO implementation you approved, not as a monthly engagement. Scope and price to follow separately.
