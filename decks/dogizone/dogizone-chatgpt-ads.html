How It Works

Two Ways In — Not Just One

📞

Standard Ads (CPC Today, oCPC Later)

  • Build one ad — title, copy, image, landing page — same as any Search or Display ad, no catalog required
  • A direct fit for DogiZone's service business — launches today as ZIP-targeted CPC traffic
  • Ready to flip into full oCPC the moment phone-call tracking ships — same campaign, no rebuild
📦

Product Feed Ads

  • Connect a Merchant Center-style catalog (100+ SKUs)
  • ChatGPT auto-generates sponsored cards directly from product data
  • A clean future opportunity for DogiMarket, queued for launch once its ecommerce platform is ready
🎯

Context-Matched Serving (Both Paths)

  • Advertiser supplies "context hints" — topics, scenarios, intent
  • OpenAI matches those to live conversation intent via auction
  • Ads labeled "Sponsored," shown below the response — never inside it
Why this matters for DogiZone: no product feed needed — a simple, ZIP-targeted CPC campaign puts DogiZone in front of ChatGPT's free-tier users today. When phone-call tracking ships, this same campaign converts straight into a full oCPC pilot, no setup lag.
