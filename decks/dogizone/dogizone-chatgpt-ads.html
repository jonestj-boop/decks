What Changed

ChatGPT Ads Just Got More Capable — and More Eligible

Ads had been quietly live since a Feb 9, 2026 pilot. Self-serve rolled out in stages — CPC bidding and a lowered spend minimum on April 10, that minimum dropped to $0 on May 5, then broadened publicly on July 22 under the "Advertise in ChatGPT" name. Two of those updates matter most for DogiZone in theory: standard ads no longer require a product catalog, and the platform can now optimize toward call and lead-form conversion events — the same ones DogiZone's paid program already runs on. In practice, neither is usable for DogiZone yet (see right).

What's New

  • → The standard ad format — title, copy, image, landing page — is open to any eligible advertiser. Product feeds are a separate, optional bulk-creation tool for large catalogs, not a requirement
  • → New oCPC (Conversion-optimized) campaigns: pick one conversion event and delivery optimizes toward it — documented events include phone call and lead submission, though not every event is live in the UI yet (see right)
  • → "Local services" is one of a short list of explicitly allowed advertiser categories during this early phase

What to Know Before Testing

  • → Ads shown only to ChatGPT Free and Go plan users — Plus, Team, and Enterprise stay ad-free
  • → Phone-call conversion tracking isn't live yet in Ads Manager — confirmed by our team directly in the platform, despite it being documented at the API level
  • → DogiZone is removing lead-form conversions from its own funnel by client decision — still present on some campaigns/pages while the site change rolls out, but not a conversion event worth building a pilot around
  • → Eligible categories are explicitly framed as "early phase" — the list could tighten or shift as the program matures

How It Works

Two Ways In — Not Just One

Product feed ads are real, but they're an optional bulk-creation tool for catalog businesses — not a gate on who can advertise. Here are both paths, and where each maps onto DogiZone.

📞

Standard Ads (CPC Today, oCPC Later)

  • Build one ad — title, copy, image, landing page — same as any Search or Display ad, no catalog required
  • Designed to fit DogiZone's service business — but DogiZone's two natural conversion events (phone call, lead submission) aren't usable right now (see below)
  • Near-term: runs as plain CPC traffic, not true oCPC, until one of those closes
📦

Product Feed Ads

  • Connect a Merchant Center-style catalog (100+ SKUs)
  • ChatGPT auto-generates sponsored cards directly from product data
  • Fits DogiMarket in theory — on hold until DogiMarket's ecommerce platform can support reliable tracking
🎯

Context-Matched Serving (Both Paths)

  • Advertiser supplies "context hints" — topics, scenarios, intent
  • OpenAI matches those to live conversation intent via auction
  • Ads labeled "Sponsored," shown below the response — never inside it
Why this matters for DogiZone: the service business doesn't need a product feed to test this channel — but it does need a working conversion event, and neither of oCPC's natural fits is ready. Phone-call tracking isn't live in Ads Manager yet, and DogiZone is deliberately removing lead-form conversions from its funnel. Until one of those changes, the honest test is plain CPC traffic, monitored manually — not a true performance pilot.

Where This Fits

Eligible, But Not Yet Ready — On Both Sides

Services and retail are each technically allowed on this channel. Neither is actually ready to run a real performance test today — for two very different reasons.

🐕

Services — ~88% of Revenue

  • Lodging/Boarding, Training, Daycare, Grooming — plus smaller Activities & Transportation lines
  • Converts via phone call — "local services" is an explicitly allowed ChatGPT ad category, with ZIP-level targeting confirmed available
  • Real constraint: neither natural oCPC event works today — phone-call tracking isn't live in Ads Manager, and DogiZone is removing lead-form conversions from its funnel
  • Path in for now: a plain CPC traffic test, monitored manually via Nimbata — not a conversion-optimized pilot
🛒

DogiMarket Retail — ~12% of Revenue

  • Dog food, accessories, gear sold through dogizone.com
  • Merchant Center feed already exists — 8 past Shopping campaigns, all currently paused, $0 spend
  • Real constraint: DogiMarket's current ecommerce platform isn't Shopify — our team is uncertain feed tracking can be reliably set up on it
  • Path in: none for now — fully paused, no spend, until the Shopify migration removes the tracking uncertainty
📋

  • Both blockers resolve on their own timelines: phone-call tracking shipping, and the Shopify migration
  • Right move: a small, low-conviction CPC test on Services only, priced to bank early-tester rates while we wait

Risks & Guardrails

What Could Go Wrong — and How We'd Catch It

The blocker here isn't eligibility or format. It's that DogiZone's own conversion setup and DogiMarket's ecommerce platform aren't ready — worth naming plainly rather than glossing over.

Real Risks

  • → No live phone-call conversion event in Ads Manager — confirmed directly, not just a documentation gap — so this can't run as a true oCPC campaign yet
  • → DogiZone is removing lead-form conversions from its funnel, and they're still live on some campaigns/pages mid-transition — the other natural conversion event is disappearing, not just unproven
  • → DogiMarket's ecommerce platform isn't Shopify — our team is uncertain feed tracking can be set up reliably on it, so Retail stays unfunded for now
  • → Without a native conversion event, results are a manual, directional read via Nimbata — not a clean CPA comparable to Google/Meta reporting

Guardrails Built Into This Plan

  • → Framed and priced as a small CPC traffic test, not a conversion-optimized pilot — no promises we can't back up yet
  • → Retail spend held entirely until the Shopify migration resolves the tracking uncertainty
  • → Test budget capped and fully isolated from the $10.2K/mo existing Google program
  • → Revisit true oCPC the moment phone-call tracking ships or a new conversion event replaces leads in DogiZone's funnel
