How we capture it

Fix, then build.

Fix what Google can't see, move the right page up, then build only what's missing.

How the work runs

Three steps, in this order.

Most roadmaps start with new articles. Yours starts with pages you already own.

Step 01

Make it findable

Point Google at the current sitemap, link to the hidden condition pages, and add canonicals, descriptions and product markup.

Step 02

Move the right page up

Add clear paths from your top guides to the shop pages that should win commercial searches, then strengthen those pages.

Step 03

Build what's missing

Add new pages where you sell the answer and your current pages don't cover the question. The plan below has 6 of these.

What we deliberately excluded

  • Other brands. 1,895 keywords, 413,080 monthly searches. You can't serve a search for someone else's product.
  • Products you don't sell. 618 keywords, 345,030 monthly searches, including finger splints, walking boots and compression socks.
  • Held for your answer. ACL, meniscus and de Quervain's terms have real volume, but your product pages don't clearly support them yet.
We kept the plan to what your catalog supports. SEMrush returned 40,977 keywords. After cutting other brands, products you don't sell, duplicates and off-topic terms, 2,012 remain. 45 are in the plan and 21 more are on the bench.

How each page is built

Existing pages keep their URLs. Each refresh gets clearer product detail, internal links from the guides that rank, and the markup listed earlier. New pages follow your clinical voice and are checked against your live product pages before they ship.

Questions for our call

Five answers that could change this plan.

Each answer opens or closes part of the work.

Do you sell a brace you recommend for de Quervain's?

About 5,400 monthly searches on the main term. Your guides mention it and your product pages don't, so we left it out. A yes opens a new page.

Should your ACL and meniscus pages go live in search?

"ACL brace" and "knee brace for meniscus tear" each show 3,600 monthly searches. Both pages list the same three general knee braces, so we held them. We can leave them out of the sitemap until you decide.

Who runs SEO and the blog today?

Your blog grew from 45 to 5,562 monthly clicks. We want to build on that work, so we need to know who owns it.

Can paid and search visitors skip the welcome page?

It affects campaign tracking and first impressions, and the fix depends on why it was added.

Do you ship internationally?

About 14% of organic clicks come from Canada, Australia and the UK, and your return policy mentions international orders. A no means we plan around US buyers only.
