Executive Summary

New creative is the fastest lever on your CPA

Across the last 7 weeks (Jun 15 – Aug 9), the account held a stable blended CPA around $0.67 on ~$130K in spend. That flat average hides the real story: your biggest employer campaigns are fatiguing while your cheapest performers sit starved of budget. Fresh creative is what closes that gap.

The challenge

  • Big spenders are fatiguing — General Motors CPA is up 20%, with Goodwill and several State-Gov campaigns climbing too
  • Spend concentrates in a few employers — DHL alone is ~$23K at a $0.81 CPA
  • Too few angles get tested — most creative is text- or SERP-based, and one ad often carries an ad set
  • Proven-cheap campaigns are underfunded — USDA Forest Service ($0.31) and Veterans Affairs ($0.49) barely spend

The opportunity

  • Refresh the fatiguing high-spenders — new video angles for GM, Goodwill, and DHL, where the dollars are
  • Templatize what already wins — the “Job Titles” angle drives your lowest CPAs on state-gov campaigns
  • Scale the cheap winners — feed budget and fresh creative to USDA, Veterans Affairs, and Coca-Cola
  • Test new hooks per employer — day-in-the-life, salary highlights, and city-based openers
  • Raise lead quality, not just cut cost — sharper hooks and clearer messaging pre-qualify the click, so more of the leads actually convert
The bottom line: high-volume UGC video turns creative testing into a repeatable engine — refresh the employers that are fatiguing, scale the ones that already convert cheap, and pull the blended CPA below $0.67 while lifting the quality of the leads it brings in.
