Phase 1

What we build

DeliverableHow it works
Landing pageNew page on your live theme at /pages/mothers-day-every-day: hero, the story behind the program, how it works, who can be nominated, the nomination form, a short FAQ, and a link to the program terms. Your team edits every word and image in the theme editor. Mobile-first, with SEO title and description and a social share image.
Nomination formBuilt on Globo's Powerful Contact Form Builder (4.9 stars, 2,500+ reviews on the Shopify App Store). Every field from the program spec: self-nomination logic that hides the honoree contact fields, photo upload, story field with a character counter, spam protection, hidden UTM capture so you can see which channel each nomination came from, and an on-page thank-you.
Short URL + entry pointsA memorable redirect (your pick: /moms, /nominate, or other), UTM-tagged links for each channel, an announcement bar link, and a homepage entry point placed with your approval.
Confirmation emailAn automatic thank-you to each nominator from a Skin Revival Science address, plus an internal notification to your team for every new nomination.
Review workflowEvery nomination lands in one shared Google Sheet with the photo viewable and status columns your team updates: New, Shortlisted, Selected, Contacted, Consent received, Shipped, Declined. Comes with a one-page how-to for reviewing, selecting, and contacting each mom.
Honoree consent + opt-inA second form on an unlisted page for each selected mom: shipping address, separate permissions for publishing her name, photo, and story, and the two people she wants to share packs with. Marketing opt-in on both forms is unchecked by default, separate from terms, and syncs to Klaviyo.
Form tool: the app plan that covers both forms, photo uploads, and the Sheet and Klaviyo sync is a $24.90/month subscription on your Shopify account. It is the only recurring cost in the build, and the account is yours from day one.

Details handled

Questions we already worked through

Where opt-ins go

Marketing opt-ins sync to Klaviyo, which is already live on your store. Intent.ly stays untouched; it handles onsite conversion, not your email list.

iPhone photos upload fine

iPhones store photos in HEIC format, and they convert to JPEG automatically on upload. We verify this on a real iPhone inside the Instagram in-app browser before launch, since that is where most nominations will come from.

Your tracking keeps working

GA4 and Meta Lead events fire on every submission through the data layer already running on your store. GTM, GA4, the Meta pixel, and Intent.ly all stay intact.

Compliance built in

We use only the copy your team supplies and check every page against the claims framework we maintain for your brand before anything publishes.
