Judgment calls

Three things we already resolved

The email platform question answers itself

The scope asks whether opt-ins sync to Klaviyo or "Intently." Intent.ly is the onsite conversion tool we installed; it holds no email list. Klaviyo is already live on the store. Opt-ins go to Klaviyo.

iPhone photos (HEIC) work

The form app accepts JPG and PNG, not HEIC, but iPhones convert HEIC to JPEG automatically when the upload field is restricted to those types. We verify this on a real iPhone inside the Instagram browser on trial day one.

Tracking rides on what we built

GA4 event and Meta Lead on submit go through the data layer that already feeds GTM and Intent.ly on this store. We installed that layer, so no risk of breaking the existing tags.

Compliance is our home turf

Client-supplied copy only, checked against the SRS claims framework we maintain (cosmetic language only, no treatment claims). A freelancer would need all of that explained first.
