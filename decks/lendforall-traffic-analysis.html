What happened to the 2025 volume: the site used to pull ~385K impressions at position 40 and a ~1% click-through — a huge pile of low-quality, deep-ranked impressions. Where we are

Content has taken these as far as it can

Here's the mechanic that matters. Getting a page from "not ranking" to page 2 is a content and on-page job — and that's done. Getting it from page 2 to page 1 is almost entirely an authority job: Google needs external signals that the site deserves the top ten for competitive, money-making terms.

The click math: position 11 (top of page 2) earns barely 1% of clicks; position 5 on page 1 earns roughly 10%. Moving a term like "loans for bad credit" (6,000+ impressions last quarter) from 14 to the top 5 isn't a small gain — it's a step-change in traffic.
The next lever

Authority — earned brand mentions & backlinks

The missing input is authority, and the way we build it is brand mentions: earned, editorial placements of LendForAll on real, relevant Canadian finance and consumer sites — genuine articles and reviews on established domains, not link-farm filler.

  • Each placement is a real site with its own audience and domain authority, with query-relevant content that points at your highest-value pages
  • We aim them surgically at the page-2 money terms — the ones already one step from page 1
  • It compounds: every month of authority lifts the whole shelf, not just one page
Why now: authority is only worth spending on once the pages are good enough to rank — pointing links at a page stuck on page 5 is wasted money. Your pages have already earned their way to page 2. This is the exact moment the investment pays.
