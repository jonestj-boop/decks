AI search presence · The entity problem

Search engines aren't sure who GTSAAS is yet.

ChatGPT, Perplexity, and Google's AI Overviews recommend brands they can resolve to one clear entity. Today, "GTSAAS" points several directions at once.

Three descriptions, one name. When third-party profiles disagree with the site, AI engines hedge or skip the brand entirely.
The fix is cheap and permanent: Organization schema with sameAs links, one canonical boilerplate pushed to every profile, and an About page that states the full name, founding team, and focus in one place.

The backlink roadmap

Earned authority, in three layers.

Defense and security buyers vet vendors hard, so every link has to hold up to scrutiny. No link farms, no private blog networks: only placements you'd be comfortable showing a procurement officer.

Layer 01 · Month 1

Reclaim & claim

Fix the 302 so legacy-domain links pass full value. Get listed on the partner and reseller pages of the technologies you represent. Align Crunchbase, LinkedIn, SAM.gov and industry directories (e.g. the Unmanned Airspace C-UAS directory) to one boilerplate.

Layer 02 · Month 2 onward

Editorial placements

Two DA40+ contextual links every month on relevant cybersecurity, infrastructure and defense-tech sites, pointed at the new hub pages. We also pitch your veteran operators as bylined experts and podcast guests.

Layer 03 · Month 3 onward

Linkable assets

One flagship resource per quarter that journalists and analysts cite: a post-quantum readiness checklist, a C-UAS buyer's guide, or an OT exposure snapshot built from public scan data.

Why partner pages matter most. The vendors you work with already rank for their own technology. A link from their partner directory is the most topically relevant, easiest-to-earn authority available to you. Most of it just needs an ask.
