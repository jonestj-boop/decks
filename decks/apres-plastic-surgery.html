You offer Botox, Dysport, Jeuveau, fillers, Sculptra, RF microneedling, BBL HEROic, MOXI, Emsculpt Neo, IV therapy, and GLP-1 weight loss. Those patients come back every three to four months. On the map, the med spas next door get them.

Why it happens and what we'd do

  • Category. Your Google profile's primary category is "Plastic surgeon." Medical spa, Skin care clinic, and Weight loss service need to be added as secondary categories
  • Services. Each injectable, laser, and wellness service listed on the profile with a short description and a link to its page
  • Posts and photos. Weekly med spa posts and before-and-after photos so the profile matches what people search for
  • Reviews that name the service. A review ask after every injectable visit, so new reviews mention "Botox" and "filler," not only surgery
Worth asking on the call: how much of the practice's revenue is med spa and weight loss compared with surgery? That decides how many of the 10 to 20 guaranteed keywords go to the med spa.
