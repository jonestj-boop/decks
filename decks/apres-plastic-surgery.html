Meta's rules for cosmetic ads are strict. No before-and-after photos in ads, 18+ only, nothing that suggests someone should feel bad about their body, and prescription weight loss needs certification. We build within those rules so the account doesn't get flagged.
Why lead forms instead of the website. Meta limits website conversion tracking for health and wellness businesses. Lead forms inside Facebook and Instagram avoid that limit, and we match leads to booked consults in your scheduling system.
