The opportunity

People who buy Revocup keep buying. The store is where first-time visitors are lost: only about 1.5% add a bag to the cart. There's no subscribe option, almost no product reviews, and the give-back story isn't on the product pages.

Why the store comes first

At your 60% margin, a new customer is worth about $95 in gross profit in their first year. On today's store, an ad-driven customer would cost around $333, so ads would lose money. The redesign has to come before the ad budget.

The plan
Step 1 · Build

Rebuild the store

Subscribe and save on every coffee, product reviews, the give-back on every page, one page per coffee with a size choice, and an Ethiopian sampler. Includes 5 automatic emails and 10 launch ads.

Step 2 · Test, 90 days

Meta and Google ads

Ads go live the week the new store launches. You approve every ad. Weekly reporting from your own Shopify, with a cost-per-customer ceiling agreed upfront.

Step 3 · Decide

Scale, hold, or stop

At day 90, scale in steps if customer cost and repeat orders are on track. If not, stop. The new store, emails, and list keep working either way.
