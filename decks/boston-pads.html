The Priority Problem

Your Homepage Is Eating Your Money Page

“Boston Apartments” is the single highest-value query in your market. Google used to rank your purpose-built listing page at #2. Now it serves the homepage instead — and the position slipped to roughly #6.

What changed on one query

Every position on this one query matters because renters convert into agent commissions — your core revenue. A slip from #2 to #6 on the market's biggest term is felt directly in the pipeline.

Why Google made the swap

  • Authority mismatch. When the target page and homepage look similarly authoritative, Google defaults to the domain root. Stronger links to the listing page break the tie in its favor
  • Intent & structure signals. Internal linking, on-page focus and schema need to say unambiguously: this page is the answer to “Boston Apartments”
  • Cannibalization. Overlapping pages competing for the same term let Google pick — and it picked wrong
The recovery move: concentrate authority (DA50+ links) and intent signals on /boston-apartments/ and your priority hubs, resolve the cannibalization, and give Google an unmistakable reason to restore the right page.
