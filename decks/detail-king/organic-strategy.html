The plan

Four moves.

In order. The first needs no outside spend and is where we'd start.

Step 01 · One page per search

Your own pages are competing with each other.

When two or three Detail King pages target the same search, Google splits the credit and none of them ranks well. Pick one page per search, point the others to it, and every link we build later lands in one place.

Step 02 · Digital PR

Earn press coverage that lifts the whole site.

Digital PR means pitching journalists who are looking for expert sources, then earning a mention and a link in the story. These are real editorial articles on established news and lifestyle sites, which carry more weight with Google and AI tools than standard link placements.

What a campaign includes

  • 10 editorial placements per campaign, landed over about 45 days
  • Story angles built around your expertise, pitched to relevant journalists
  • Links back to detailking.com using your brand name
  • Any placement that drops in the first 12 months gets replaced
  • From you: a spokesperson, a short bio and topics to steer toward
Why it helps your product pages: press links build authority for the whole domain. That authority flows from the homepage through your collections to the product pages, so it supports the targeted links on the previous slide.

Step 04 · Shift the ad budget

As rankings climb, ads move to new customers.

Ads keep doing the heavy lifting for new-customer growth. The change is that they stop paying for clicks you can earn for free.

Later: lower bids as pages reach the top three

What to expect

What the next six months look like.

Links take time to be found and counted. Their effect tends to last longer than ad clicks, which stop when spend stops.

Weeks 1–4

Page fixes

One lead page per search for Mytee, Tornador, Rupes and Koch Chemie. Link-gap report, tracking baseline for every target search, and a digital PR spokesperson and angles agreed.

Months 2–3

Links go live

First backlinks and brand mentions published to Tier 1 pages. The first digital PR campaign lands its placements over about 45 days. Early movement usually shows on the closest pages first, the ones already at 7–8.

Months 4–6

Rankings move, ads adjust

Tier 1 pages climbing toward the top three. Bid tests start on searches that hold. Tier 2 pages get their links.

What's realistic. These are other brands' products, so the manufacturer's site and Amazon often hold positions 1–2. The goal is to move Tier 1 pages from 7–10 into the top three to five. On this year's data, a top-three spot gets about twice the click rate of positions 7–10. Position 1 won't be possible on every search.
