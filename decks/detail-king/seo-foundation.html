Owner decisions come first. For each split search on slide 10 we pick the page that should rank, point the other page's links to it, and, where two collections do the same job (Wash Brushes inside Exterior Brushes, the two DeWalt collections), merge them with a redirect. Every merge goes to you for approval before anything changes. Each merge retires one URL: we delete it, redirect it to the page that stays, and point menus, links and older redirects straight there.
