The Situation

Real revenue, decided blind

There's a genuine business under this account — $603.5K in reported purchase value on ~$1,800/month of spend. But four problems are compounding, and each one is hiding the others.

What's working

  • — Strong, healthy repeat business — a real asset most accounts would envy
  • — Headline testing already in place (5 live options) with intent-matched CTAs
  • — Consistent spend discipline — nothing runaway to unwind
  • — Purchase volume held through most of the year

What's holding it back

  • — Broken tracking — the Pixel goes quiet exactly where the funnel begins
  • — A leaky funnel — steep drop-off from cart to checkout across every ad set
  • — Creative fatigue — one static ad per ad set, unchanged for 17 months
  • — No new-vs-returning read — can't tell acquisition from re-conversion
The through-line: everything downstream — creative, structure, audience strategy — is being decided blind until tracking and segmentation are rebuilt. That's where we start.

The Diagnosis

Four problems, one root cause

Fixing the visible symptoms first would just move the guesswork around. These resolve in order — each one unlocks a clean read on the next.

01 · Tracking integrity

The Pixel goes quiet where the funnel begins

Page View doesn't fire reliably on the homepage or category pages — only 60% of paid clicks register a Landing Page View. Add to Cart and Checkout Initiated carry no conversion value. The 45.2× ROAS can't be trusted at face value.

02 · Funnel & UX friction

The path to purchase isn't easy to follow

Even where tracking works, drop-off between Add to Cart and Checkout is steep across all three ad sets. It shows up in the numbers where the Pixel is firing correctly — a UX signal, not just a measurement artifact.

03 · Creative fatigue

One ad per ad set, unchanged since March 2025

A single image asset, no video in rotation, running for 17 months. Frequency climbed from ~4.8 to ~6.1 right as CTR collapsed. Meta's delivery needs creative diversity to find and hold an efficient audience.

04 · New vs. returning

The account can't see who it's actually reaching

Customer Segments aren't configured, so every breakdown returns "Unknown." Advantage+ layers a customer list and a lookalike with no exclusions — structurally biased toward re-reaching buyers, not finding new ones.

Our Approach

Fix what you're measuring before what you're spending on

Three workstreams, run in sequence. Sequence matters more than speed — each one makes the next diagnosable.

📡

Measurement rebuild

  • Restore Page View across the full funnel, not just cart-adjacent pages
  • Rebuild Add to Cart & Checkout events with conversion values
  • Server-side / CAPI validation and Event Match Quality lift
  • Configure Customer Segments (New / Existing / Engaged)
🎯

Acquisition structure

  • Split acquisition and retention into distinct campaigns
  • Add exclusions for existing customers on prospecting
  • Rebuild audience signals around net-new discovery
  • Reduce Add to Cart → Checkout friction in the path
🎨

Creative engine

  • Rebuild the image and video library from scratch
  • Establish a refresh cadence so nothing runs 17 months again
  • Tighten descriptions to fit the visible character limit
  • Keep the headline testing that's already showing intent
