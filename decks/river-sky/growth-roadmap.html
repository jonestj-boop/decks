The structural problem

Your store is competing against itself.

Years of seasonal launches and Shopify duplication have left many versions of the same page. Google cannot tell which dress collection or which Brooklyn dress is the real one, so it splits your authority across all of them and ranks none of them well. This is the first thing to fix, and most of it is cleanup, not new content.

AI search presence

The same gap shows up when she asks AI.

The collections problem and the AI problem are the same problem seen twice. When a shopper asks Google's AI or ChatGPT for the best sustainable, made-in-USA tie-dye dresses, it names brands that look exactly like you, and leaves you out.

River+Sky is not named in any of them. On the first query you appear only in the paid Sponsored row, for your Juniper dress, while the free answer above it goes to the others. This is the finding from your AEO brief, and it has the same root cause: AI reads your site and the web, finds thin category evidence and no third-party mentions, and recommends the brands that have both.
Why SEO and AEO move together. The category collection pages we build for search are the same pages AI reads to decide who to name. The clean product pages and review schema are the structured facts it quotes. The roundup and Reddit mentions are the outside proof it trusts. One body of work lifts you in both places at once.

The plan, in three workstreams

Collections, products, then content and conversion.

The order is deliberate. Your money pages carry the most upside and most of the early work is fixing what you already have, which pays back faster than anything written from scratch.

Workstream 01

Collections as landing pages

Turn your category pages into pages that can rank. One canonical page per real intent (maxi dresses, tie-dye, made-in-USA, SUNDoWN, sweatshirts), with genuine intro copy, internal links, and schema. Consolidate the duplicate and seasonal collections into it. This is where head-term demand lands.

Workstream 02

Product pages that earn and convert

Decide how your 32 Brooklyn listings should rank, and redirect retired colorways instead of deleting them, so rankings and links stop scattering. Unique descriptions, product and review schema, and internal links to the parent collection. Every fix helps search and conversion together.

Workstream 03

Supporting content and CRO

Buying guides that capture research demand and route it into the collections, the third-party mentions that feed AI, and conversion work on the money pages themselves: reviews shown up top, made-in-USA trust signals, and cleaner merchandising. Traffic and conversion, not one without the other.

Why this plan

Why the fixes start first.

The sequence is the strategy. Here is what we prioritized, what we deliberately left for later, and what we confirm with your data before scaling.

What we prioritized

Money pages first

Collections and products are where intent lands and where the sale happens. Set aside the catch-all clothing grid, and your dress, sweatshirt and product pages earn under 15% of your organic traffic. Fixing them is the highest-return work on the site.

Cleanup before creation

Consolidating duplicate collections and product URLs recovers rankings and links you already earned. That pays back faster than writing anything new, so it goes first.

One effort, two engines

The category pages, clean products, and outside links all serve traditional search and AI answers at once. We are not building an SEO plan and an AEO plan, we are building one asset that works in both.

What we sequenced after, and why

  • Articles before fixes. Publishing before the collections can receive the traffic would waste it. The articles start as the fixes land, so each one links into a page built to sell.
  • Head terms owned by giants. We target the specific, winnable category terms, not "dresses" in the abstract. Small, credible brands hold this ground, which is why merit wins it.
  • Precise forecasts. Until Google Search Console is connected, every number here is a Semrush estimate. We would rather hand you honest direction now and exact targets once we see your real queries.
What GSC unlocks on day one. Your true page-and-query traffic, the terms you already rank for in striking distance, and which pages convert. That is when this directional plan becomes a scored, prioritized one.
