The proposal

How we'd run it.

Meta and Google, managed as one account plan, with separate budgets for new customers, warm visitors and past buyers.

Meta · The plan

Split the account by funnel stage so each audience gets its own budget.

Your retargeting audiences are already segmented well: leads, engagers and past buyers, with clean exclusions. We keep that work and give it room to run.

Prospecting

Net-new customers only

Define an engaged audience at the account level and exclude it, along with purchasers, so cold spend stays cold. That gives you a real new-customer CAC to manage against.

Retargeting

Warm visitors who didn't buy

Move leads and engagers out of the shared CBO campaign into their own budget. Switch the lifecycle setting off "all audiences" and cap bids so the small pools don't throttle.

Retention

The 40% who buy again

Win-back and repeat-purchase get protected budget and their own creative. With a $180–200 AOV and a 40% repeat base, this is the cheapest revenue in the account.

Creative that looks like River + Sky: the add-to-cart drop says the ads need to get people to product faster. We'll build from your own product photography, lifestyle shoots and customer content. We'll test angles like Made in USA, seasonal edits and fit, with separate creative for your 35–55 and 55+ customers, and put the budget behind your top-selling dresses and sweatshirts each season. Nothing that looks AI-made, because the handmade feel is the brand.
Google · The plan

Win back your brand searches first, then scale what's working.

Google is under 15% of paid spend today. It should be the cheapest channel you run, because a lot of that demand is people already looking for River + Sky.

Week 1

Reset Brand Search

Bring the ROAS target down to a number the campaign can clear, or move to impression-share bidding, and get back toward the 85% share you had in May.

Weeks 2–4

Win the Shopping row

Your Juniper dress already shows in sponsored product results, with competitors listed right under it. We reset Brooklyn Dress to a realistic target, clean up feed titles and images for top sellers, and add negatives from search-term data.

Month 2+

Scale non-brand

Catch-All is healthy. Once brand is covered, we add budget there and test non-brand Search on the product terms where it earns its keep.

Cleanup: two paused campaigns (DemandGen Video and Campaign #1) get retired or rebuilt so the account only carries campaigns with a job to do.

Quick fixes first, then rebuild, then scale.
