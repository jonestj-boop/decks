Automation 1

Clean up the inbox

Start here. Patients who reply and hear nothing are more likely to miss a visit. Some of the current queue may be simple confirmations, so sort it before judging the backlog.

A

One-time cleanup

  • In the Communication Center, filter Reply Status to Needs Reply and Conversation to Open
  • Reply to real questions and reschedule requests first, oldest first
  • Close completed threads with Done. Closing doesn't delete the history.
B

Settings

  • In the Appointment Reminders Campaign under Special Settings, review "When a patient responds to an appointment reminder campaign message with C, 1, or 2, mark the message thread as unread." If confirmations are cluttering the inbox, turn it off after confirming what "2" does.
  • Turn on Replies for Missed Messages and Replies for Missed Calls under Settings > Communications
  • Under Automation and Closure Rules, review the 45-day defaults. Don't let the Unresponded rule stand in for answering patients.
  • Assign conversations to the inbox owner
New routine: check Needs Reply at opening and again after lunch, and answer patient messages the same business day.

  • Delivery: Text Preferred, so email is the fallback where it's available
  • Send window: set this to your office's preferred hours under Reminder Timing
  • Special Settings: turn on Family Messaging, Send Confirmation Notification, Send Call Back Notification and "Include the day of the week"

  • Monitor: check the Not Sent tab weekly for missing contact details and other failures

Short-notice bookings won't receive the earlier reminders. Same-day reminders must go out at least 2 hours before the appointment.

Automation 3

New patient forms

Finished forms mean a smoother first visit, and they give the team an early signal about who is likely to show up.

  • Confirm that new patient forms send automatically when a new patient is booked
  • The day before each visit, check Pending Forms and use Send Reminder for anyone who hasn't finished
  • Treat unfinished forms plus an unconfirmed appointment as the top priority for a phone call
  • Use Mark as Submitted only when a form was completed on paper or outside the system
  • Check the Not Sent tab for missing or invalid contact details
Automation 4

Google review requests

Menu: Campaigns > Review Request Campaign. Practice by Numbers' help articles list it under both General and Follow-Up Campaigns.

A

Connect and tag

  • Check the Google connection under New Settings > Reputation > Review Request Settings
  • Run Settings > Marketing > Tag Patients Who Have Written Reviews one time
  • Check the tagged list on the Reputation page. Tag patients by hand where Google and Dentrix names don't match.
B

Campaign settings

  • Audience: all patients with completed appointments
  • Exclude: check "Exclude the patients who already reviewed"
  • Frequency: no more than one request per patient in 12 months, using the past-year exclusion
  • Households: turn on "Exclude even if a patient with the same contact information got contacted"
  • Channel: Text Preferred, with a send window during business hours
  • Timing: as soon after the visit as the account allows, ideally 1–2 hours
  • Follow-up: one reminder 3 days later if the patient didn't click
  • Review site: send patients straight to Google if the selection page allows it
  • Use the Test Date option, send a test to a staff phone, then set the campaign to Active
Keep the profile safe: leave "Give patient an option to send feedback directly to you" off unless it's clear this doesn't steer unhappy patients away from Google. Don't use offers in review invites, and don't exclude patients because they might be unhappy.
  • New review alerts: turn on Google Business Profile notifications for the reply owner, and also use any alert available in Practice by Numbers
  • In person: whoever sees the patient keeps asking, and patients leave the review on their own phones
  • Staff drawing: it's fine to reward staff, but nothing is offered to patients

Settings

  • Under Edit Filters, include failed appointments as well as cancelled ones

Automation 6

Unscheduled treatment, starting with implants

This matches your priority of higher-value, better-quality cases. Start with implants and tooth replacement, then expand once the sequence is working. Menu: Revenue IQ Follow-Up, or Campaigns > Unscheduled Treatment Campaign.

  • Audience: filter Service Code(s) to the implant and tooth replacement codes Dr. Locklear approves
  • Provider: use Provider(s) if some providers should be handled separately
  • Starting schedule: day 3 text and email, day 10 text, day 21 call task, day 45 final text
  • Stop rule: messages stop when the patient schedules a procedure from the treatment plan
  • Message: keep it general, such as "We'd love to help you with your next step." Save treatment details for the phone or the office.

Message rules

How to keep this from feeling like too many texts

  • Priority: appointment reminders come first, then missed-visit recovery, treatment follow-up, recall, reviews and reactivation
  • Spacing: avoid more than one non-appointment message to a patient in the same week where the account allows it
  • Booked patients: every follow-up stops once the patient has an appointment
  • Opt-outs: these apply across every campaign. Don't place STOP or similar keywords in message text unless Practice by Numbers advises it for your account.
  • Privacy: keep treatment details out of messages
Planned closures: our notes show Nov 19, Nov 23–26, Dec 23–24 and Dec 28–31. Once confirmed, don't launch batches around those dates, and set an after-hours reply saying when the office reopens.
