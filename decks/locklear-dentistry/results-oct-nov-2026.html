Working with paid ads

Ads bring the calls. Local search builds what you don't pay for.

In October, your ads brought in 106 calls straight from the ads and 84 from the website. Cost per call ran about $20.63, and new patients averaged $859 in treatment value in the first half of the month.

Your profile and website add patients on top of that without a cost per click. When someone sees an ad and then checks your profile, your reviews can help decide whether they call you or another practice.

What the program adds

  • Practice by Numbers, fully set up: a review request after every completed visit, follow-ups, and patients who already reviewed are left out
  • A past-patient campaign: asking patients from the last 12 to 18 months, in weekly batches
  • Your team asking in person, with patients leaving reviews on their own phones
  • Replies to every review, written to protect patient privacy
  • A monthly review report alongside this one

The full plan is in the review program and Practice by Numbers workflow. Fixing missed appointments with the front desk script also helps you keep more of the patients your ads already bring in.
