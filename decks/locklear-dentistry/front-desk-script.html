New patient front desk script

Locklear Dentistry · Goal: at least 75% of booked new patients show up

1

When they call to book

Offer the soonest opening first
Book it soon"Thanks for calling Locklear Dentistry, this is [your name]. I can get you in as soon as [day and time]. Would that work for you?"
Set cost expectations"Before I lock that in, we're a fee-for-service office and we file your insurance claim for you. We also accept CareCredit. Do you have any questions about cost?"
Make it feel reserved"I've set aside [day] at [time] with [doctor or hygienist] just for you. Does that time definitely work? We'll text you a reminder."
2

Confirmation call

Within 1 day of booking, and again the day before
If they answer"Hi [first name], this is [your name] at Locklear Dentistry, confirming [day] at [time]. Are you still good for that? Any questions before your visit?"
If they can't make it"No problem at all. Let's find a better time. I have [option 1] or [option 2]. Which is easier?"
Voicemail"Hi, this is [your name] from Locklear Dentistry for [first name], about [day] at [time]. Please call us back at (336) 282-4012."
3

Reminder texts

Only to patients who agreed to receive texts
  • At booking: "Locklear Dentistry: You're booked for [day] at [time]. Need to change it? Call (336) 282-4012."
  • 2–3 days before, and the day before: "Locklear Dentistry: See you [day] at [time]. Reply C to confirm. Need a different time? Call (336) 282-4012."
  • If there's no reply by the day before, call them
Keep it private
  • Never mention treatment or the reason for the visit in a voicemail or text
  • No guilt about missed visits. Keep it friendly and easy to come back.
4

If they don't show

Call within 1 hour, then in 2 days, then in 1 week
Call"Hi [first name], it's [your name] at Locklear Dentistry. We missed you today and wanted to make sure everything's okay. I'd be glad to find a better time. I have [option 1] or [option 2]."
Ask why"Was there anything that got in the way? I want to make it easier next time."
Voicemail"Hi [first name], it's [your name] at Locklear Dentistry. We missed you today. Call us at (336) 282-4012 and we'll find a better time."
If they say…
"How much will it cost?""Your first visit is $______. We file your insurance claim for you, and we accept CareCredit. Would you like me to walk you through it?"
"Do you take my insurance?""We're fee-for-service and we file the claim for you. What you pay depends on your plan, so I'm happy to look into it for you before your visit."
"I'm nervous about the dentist.""You're not alone, and thanks for telling me. I'll let the team know so they can walk you through everything first."
"I need to check my schedule.""Of course. Can I hold [day and time] and call you tomorrow to confirm?"
