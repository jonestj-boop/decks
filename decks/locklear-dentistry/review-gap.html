Why it matters

Reviews decide who gets the call

  • Google weighs how many reviews a practice has, and how recent they are, when choosing the top 3 map results
  • People who find you through your ads can check your reviews before deciding to call
  • Your profile drew 590 direction requests in Oct–Nov, about 7 times last year

Reviews aren't the only factor. Distance and how well the profile matches the search matter too, which is why some practices with fewer reviews still rank ahead.
