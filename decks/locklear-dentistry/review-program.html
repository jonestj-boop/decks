What we do

Setup · Weeks 1–2 after access

  • Tune up your Google Business Profile
  • Set up automatic review requests by text and email after each completed appointment
  • Check that your existing QR cards and signs go straight to your Google review page
  • Train your team in a 30-minute session

Launch · Weeks 3–6

  • Ask patients seen in the last 12 to 18 months for a review, in 4 weekly batches

Every month

  • Reply to new reviews within 48 hours, and negative reviews within 24
  • Check that requests are still going out, and fix any problems
  • Send a one-page report with new reviews, rating and competitor counts
What we need from your team
  • Manager access to your Google Business Profile
  • Admin access to your patient messaging software, and confirmation that patients agreed to receive texts
  • A signed Business Associate Agreement before we access patient data
  • Your past-patient list and monthly patient volume
  • One point person, usually the office manager
  • A quick in-person ask from your team at every visit
How we protect your profile
  • Every patient gets the same request, not only the happy ones
  • No rewards or discounts for leaving a review
  • No reviews from staff or family, or from office devices
  • Replies never confirm someone is a patient or mention treatment
