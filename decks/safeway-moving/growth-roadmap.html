Why this plan

Why these five pillars, and what we left out.

The selection is the work. Here is the reasoning behind it.

The pillars

What a Move Costs · 14 articles

Your biggest page-two cluster and the ground you cede to forums. Reddit ranks first on every version of how much do movers cost. It is the smallest pillar because most of its keywords turned out to be pages you already rank for, which moved to the refresh list where they earn faster.

Moving State to State · 26 articles

Interstate work is the business. Your existing route guides average position 17 while your neighborhood guides average 12, which is a format problem rather than a market problem.

Where You Are Landing · 45 articles

The one thing already working. Your 43 neighborhood guides sit at position 12 and convert their impressions at a higher rate than your 174 state pages or your service pages. This pillar scales the format that already earns for you, and it covers both hubs.

Getting It There Safely · 23 articles

Packing queries put you at positions 54 to 64 on real demand. Specialty work is the opposite: you rank first for piano movers near me. This pillar answers the question underneath both, which is whether your things arrive intact.

Business & Corporate Moves · 12 articles

The smallest pillar and the highest value per deal. Search Console shows 2,908 commercial queries producing 12,401 impressions and zero clicks, with commercial movers near me sitting at position three. You rank and collect nothing.

What we deliberately excluded

  • Generic find-a-mover queries · 157 keywords · 1,634,220. Moving company at 550,000, movers near me at 110,000. The map pack owns these. Comparison queries like best long distance moving companies stayed in, because an article can win those.
  • Outside what you sell · 1315 keywords · 1,544,260. Car shipping (277,960), DIY containers, truck rental and packing supplies (637,270), standalone self-storage (231,490) and international moves (170,880). If car shipping is wrong, tell us on the call and it comes back in.
  • Competitor brand names · 1412 keywords · 1,065,400. Someone searching a rival by name is not looking for you.
  • Variants that collapse into one target · 3496 keywords · 875,140. Long distance movers and long distance moving companies are one article, not two.
  • Plus keywords you already rank for, your own brand, and junk fragments. 832 keywords moved to the Refresh First tab, including twenty-two caught mid-build by the same check we run on the bench. Full detail in the Excluded tab.
Raw research surfaced 11,712,660 in monthly search volume. We are showing you 3,360,880. The biggest single piece of the difference is 2,867,560 of long-tail variations we kept as overflow rather than plan targets. The largest deliberate cuts are listed on this slide. Where your buyers read · The plan

Five placements a month, aimed at the corridors you're already building.

Not brand advertising. Real answers, in the threads buyers already trust more than a mover's own site.

How it works

Aged accounts, not the brand

Placements run through established, unflagged accounts. Nothing posts from an account that reads as a company account, because that is what gets removed on sight.

Five a month to start

Minimal on purpose. A sudden flood of mentions reads as manufactured. Five lets this stay native while we learn which threads and subreddits actually hold, with room to scale once it is working.

Answers, not pitches

Every placement responds to a real question someone asked, the way an experienced mover would answer a neighbor. Safeway comes up because it is relevant, not because it is being sold.

Dual-traction targets only

Placements go where a thread or subreddit already shows both search and AI-citation activity. A placement nobody sees does not seed anything.

What we deliberately left off the list

  • r/moving. Moderated by people inside the industry, by your own account, and closed to your posts historically. Re-testing a channel you have already spent years failing to break into is not where the first five placements go.
  • Generic national moving subreddits with no route specificity. They compete for the same ground your content pillars already target. Corridor and city threads carry less competition and match the pillar you are already building in "Where You Are Landing."
You start ahead here. By your own count, three or four negative threads in years, against an industry you described as carrying "a ton." That is a cleaner baseline than most movers have, worth protecting rather than testing carelessly.

Every target, approved before it posts

The subreddit and thread list is pulled live and reviewed with you before the first placement goes out, and again every month after. Reddit communities and their moderation shift constantly, so a list locked today would be stale by the time it ran. You see every target before it is used, not after.

No guarantees, and we are not pretending otherwise. A moderator can remove a placement at any time, for any reason, including bias toward a competitor. That risk does not go away. What changes is the volume of attempts and the quality of the accounts making them.
It reinforces the content plan, it does not replace it. The 321,600 monthly searches from the previous slide get attacked two ways at once: your own pages climbing through the cost pillar, and native presence in the forum threads already answering the same question.
