What you're buying

One placement, defined

  • A written article on an established site (DR 30+, real organic traffic), with a contextual link to one of your service pages sitting in the body copy — not a footer, sidebar or directory listing
  • Topic matched to your audience: applications, admissions, graduate study, careers
  • Target page and anchor chosen from the backlink plan, so authority lands on the pages that are closest to ranking — your service hubs and striking-distance pages
  • Live URL delivered in the monthly report with each site's DR and traffic
How this differs from brand mentions: a backlink's job is authority — it points at your pages so Google ranks them higher, measured in referring domains and positions. A brand mention's job is the recommendation itself — your name inside buyer's-guide content that AI engines pull answers from. The two programs run in parallel and don't overlap: different sites, different content, different measurement.
