On our call we put it this way: content is the ground war — your blog gives Google pages worth ranking. Backlinks are the artillery behind it — the authority that lets those pages win their positions. Brand mentions are the air war — being named in the sources AI engines read, so when an applicant skips Google entirely and asks ChatGPT who to hire, you're in the answer. Each one feeds the others; none replaces another.

What to expect, honestly

Nobody can promise a specific ranking or that a specific engine names you for a specific query — be skeptical of anyone who says otherwise. What we commit to is the placements: how many, the site quality, where they point. The pattern across our partners: links index and referring domains climb in the first 30 to 60 days, position movement on target pages follows at 60 to 90, and the effect compounds because every link keeps working after it's placed. Your starting line makes progress easy to see — these 38 sites link to your competitors a combined dozens of times and to you zero.
