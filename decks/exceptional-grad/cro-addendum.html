Workstream A · Phase 2 · extends original finding 8

One approved spec per product, everywhere

The audit confirmed that service pages, product pages, metadata and schema contradict each other on prices, hours, school counts and edit limits (UX-03). The fix is not nine spot-edits; it is one approved specification per SKU, then propagation.

2

Build the product matrix with the client

UX-03Blocks on client decisions

One row per SKU: audience, document type, document count or word limit, number of schools, edit rounds, advisor hours, term/cycle, turnaround start condition, and price. EmberTribe collects the decisions from the client (see Decisions section); the contractor builds the record and treats it as the single source for every surface below.

3

Resolve the confirmed mismatches

UX-03UX-04
  • Graduate personal-essay page shows Standard $1,650 / Elite $2,550 while five other graduate service pages show $1,950 / $2,750 for the same package destinations — render the approved prices from shared product data in every card
  • Graduate Professional $3,450: essay page says two advising hours plus one interview-prep hour; five other pages say three virtual-advising hours — apply the client's decision and synchronize
  • Undergraduate Secondary Essay Editing "1 University" promises three universities in title, meta description and long description — correct H1, metadata, body and schema to the approved entitlement
  • Medical interview preparation sold as "2 Hours" while the copy promises two detailed 2-hour sessions — state total hours and session structure separately
  • Medical advising "2 Hours" has title/meta advertising a one-hour session — correct the duration everywhere
  • Three-program law supplemental-essay product advertises five programs in title and description — correct metadata to the approved count
  • Job resume/CV editing copy repeatedly promises personal-essay refinement — rewrite short description, long description and metadata to the real deliverable
  • Graduate review $1,850 "Unlimited" card also lists "Resume/CV - 3 Edits" and "Personal Statement - 3 Edits" — state exactly what is unlimited and what keeps edit limits
  • MBA Unlimited resume metadata promises "up to three edits"; MBA five-program supplemental meta description says three programs — correct both to the approved spec
  • Put the approved "unlimited" boundaries and turnaround definition (when the clock starts, business days or not) beside the price and CTA on each affected product, not only in the termsUX-04 · wording comes from the client's approved policy

The original scope had no accessibility items. The audit's sampled Lighthouse checks (scores 86–97) confirmed nine defects:

9

Fix the sampled defects, then regression-test the templates

A11Y-01 … A11Y-09

  • Review iframes lack descriptive titles on service, contact and hub templates A11Y-02
