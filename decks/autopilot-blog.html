How we read it

  • Month 1 is for learning. Expect a higher cost per demo while targeting and creative settle
  • Month 2 should get cost per demo under target on at least one channel
  • Month 3 decides: double the winner, cut the loser, or rework the offer
  • Every week you get a channel and vertical breakdown from ad to paid customer
  • With our $2,500 fee included, this example month costs about $1,830 to $2,750 per customer. At $500 a month in revenue, that pays back in four to six months. Our fee doesn't change with spend during the test
The pilot is the lever. If more pilots convert, the same spend brings in more customers. Shorter pilots, a clear day-7 checkpoint, and a customer story in the follow-up all help.
Direct vs. resellers

Run both, but price them so they don't compete.

Resellers want a lower price in exchange for volume. That's a good trade if the floor protects your margin and resellers can't undercut your direct price to the same buyer.

Direct to SMB

Your main engine

  • Full price, $300 to $1,000 a month
  • Brought in by ads and the site, closed on demos
  • Managed onboarding is part of the value
  • Needs a hire or partner for SEO review as volume grows
Resellers

Volume with less support

  • Wholesale price per blog with a hard floor, plus volume tiers at 10, 25, and 50 blogs
  • The reseller handles client support and you handle the engine
  • White-label option for agencies, co-branded for software vendors
  • Minimum resale price so partners don't undercut your direct offer
Our recommendation: launch direct first with the site and ads. Write up reseller terms now from your two existing partners, then start reaching out to agencies after the 90-day test, once the site and case studies exist to send them to.
