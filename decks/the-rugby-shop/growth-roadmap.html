Why this plan

Why this plan

Four pillars built on what you sell and who you serve: players, coaches, referees and the parents of new players.

The pillars

Positions and gear · 27 articles

All 15 positions, what each needs on match day, and gear care: boots, studs, mouthguards, scrum caps, socks and washing kit.

Learn the game · 31 articles

Rules, scoring, the scrum, ruck, maul and lineout, written for new players and the parents on the touchline.

Training and coaching · 23 articles

Drills, tackling, passing and kicking, agility, speed and conditioning, warm-ups and cool-downs: the sessions your tackle bags, cones and ladders are built for.

Youth, clubs and referees · 13 articles

Youth programs, Rookie Rugby and tag rugby, joining a club, sponsorship and fundraising for teams, and becoming a referee: the grassroots side of the game.

What we deliberately excluded

  • Match news and fan searches · 2.3M searches/mo. World Cup fixtures, scores, national teams and players. Big numbers, but nobody is shopping.
  • Rugby league, other sports, the town of Rugby · 560K searches/mo. Different codes, fishing tackle bags, Rugby, North Dakota, other languages and similar.
  • Fashion rugby shirts · 501K searches/mo. Ralph Lauren style striped shirts and polos, not playing kit.
  • One-word searches, college teams, other brands and trivia · 360K searches/mo. The one-word search “rugby” (about 105K) and other fragments, single college programs, Adidas, Nike or Gilbert gear, American football and hockey gear, near-me club finders, history and injury stories.
Raw research found 4.37M searches a month. After cutting what you don't sell, 473,970 remain. We'd rather plan on that.

The strategy

1 · Make the name unmistakable

Every article carries “The Rugby Shop North America”, your BC base and Canada and US shipping, so the engines stop mixing you up with the UK store. Built into the content above at no extra cost.

2 · Get listed where AI looks

Placements in “best of” lists and reviews on established sites (DR 30 to 60, 1,000+ monthly visitors) for the questions buyers ask AI: where to buy rugby boots in Canada, the best rugby gear stores in the US, the best ball for a new player. Each one goes live in about 15 to 30 days.

3 · Measure it every month

We rerun the SEMrush AI visibility report and a fixed set of buying prompts in ChatGPT, Gemini and Google AI Mode, so you see which mentions the engines picked up.
