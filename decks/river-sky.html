The shift

Your buyer increasingly asks AI before she asks Google

Answer Engine Optimization (AEO) is the work of being the brand AI names when someone asks a buying question. It matters now for a very specific reason: more of your customers start with ChatGPT, Google's AI Overviews, Gemini and Perplexity, and those tools do not return ten blue links. They return a short list of names. If you are not on the list, you are not in the consideration set at all. There is no page two.

  • AI does not pick the brand with the biggest ad budget. It picks the brand it has the most trustworthy, consistent evidence about, drawn from the brand's own site and, more importantly, from what others say about it.
  • That makes AEO the opposite of Meta in one crucial way: it is an owned, compounding asset. Meta visibility stops the day you stop paying. An AI recommendation you earn keeps working while you sleep.
  • For a niche, high-quality brand like yours it is the great leveler. You do not need to outspend anyone; you need to be the most clearly, credibly documented answer to a specific question.
The plan

Three inputs, run together: content, Reddit, ads

Getting River+Sky into that answer, and turning the attention into sales, takes three things working as one system. Any one alone underperforms. Together they compound.

1

Content: give AI something to quote

The story that wins this category (California-made, eco-friendly fabric, hand-dyed by artisans, the “back to our roots” origin) barely exists on your site as text AI can read. We build the product and editorial pages that state it plainly, so the answer engines have River+Sky evidence to cite.

2

Reddit & third-party mentions: earn the trust

AI decides who to recommend based on outside consensus: Reddit threads, “best sustainable tie-dye brands” roundups, boutique and press mentions. MATE the Label lives in those. River+Sky does not, yet. We change that with genuine, useful placements the answer engines already license and cite.

3

Ads: capture and scale the demand

Your $35K on Meta stays the engine for demand and retargeting, and we add search/shopping capture for the exact buying queries above. Ads reach her now; content and Reddit make every ad dollar convert better because she also sees you in the answer, the roundup and the thread.

How it compounds. Content gives AI accurate River+Sky facts to quote. Reddit and roundups give it the outside proof it needs to feel safe naming you. Ads put you in front of the buyer today and feed better landing pages and audiences back into the machine. She asks AI, reads a thread, sees a roundup, then gets your ad, and every touch says the same thing. That is when a niche brand scales without just spending more.

Input three: ads

Keep the Meta engine, and stop leaving the free spot on the table

None of this replaces paid. It makes paid work harder. Your $35K on Meta is how you create and capture demand at scale today, and we would run it as the performance engine it is. Two additions close the loops this brief exposes.

  • Search & shopping capture. On the query we tested, you already show in the paid row while the organic and AI spots go to competitors. We make sure the paid capture is deliberate and efficient for the high-intent buying terms, while content earns the free spots underneath so you stop paying full freight for every visit.
  • Content-fed Meta. The brand-story and review-driven pages become better landing pages, retargeting hooks and creative angles. Same $35K, more of it converting, because the message is consistent from ad to answer to thread.
  • De-risk the dependency. A brand living almost entirely on Meta is one CAC spike from a hard quarter. Earned AI and organic visibility is the owned asset that lowers blended acquisition cost over time.
