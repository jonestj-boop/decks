What this means for Square One

A score of 14 out of 100. That puts you in the band where a brand is effectively invisible in AI answers, which is where most independent clinics sit today. It is an early market.
The reading is not that AI ignores you. It is the opposite. 165 of your pages have been good enough to cite. Your pages do identify the clinic, the address and the town, so the entity is there. What is missing is the layer that ties a specific answer to you: question and answer markup on the pages being cited, and a named clinician attached to the claim.
What changes it. FAQ markup on the pages that already answer questions, a named practitioner with real credentials on each clinical page, and internal links that put a Fort Collins treatment page one click from every cited article. Your business schema is already in good shape, on 83 of the 100 pages we checked, so this is an addition rather than a rebuild.
