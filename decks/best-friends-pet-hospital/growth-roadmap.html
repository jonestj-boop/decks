Why these five pillars

Each one starts from a service on your site. A few articles explain procedures you may not offer, so owners comparing options still find you.

Surgery, orthopedics and limping · 34 articles

TTA repair, FHO, knee cap and fracture surgery, plus limping.

  • Town searches. Google gives these to clinics in that town. Your service pages cover them.
