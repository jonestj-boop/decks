How Every Rewrite Is Made

A Hybrid Approach: Human-Written, AI-Assisted

You said you don't want everything AI-written — and you won't. Here's our exact process: a person writes every page, with AI assisting research and optimization. Three people and a uniqueness check touch every piece.

1

SEO Strategist

  • Identifies low-competition opportunities with our proprietary scoring
  • Decides what's worth rewriting first
Human-led
2

Researcher

  • Builds a detailed brief + outline to outrank the current winners
  • Uses AI here to surface angles & competitor gaps — it informs the brief, writes nothing
AI assists research
3

Writer — a person

  • Composes the article from the brief, your notes & real references
  • AI surfaces semantic & internal-link suggestions in the background; the words are the writer's
Human writes it
4

Editor

  • Reviews for readability, accuracy & fit with your brief
  • Runs a Copyscape uniqueness check before it ever reaches you
Human-led
Net: the "AI content" your specialist warned about — generic, unedited, published at scale — is exactly what this process is built to avoid. Google rewards helpful, expert content; that's what a human writer + human editor produce.
