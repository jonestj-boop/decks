How it works

What a brand mention actually is

Not link-farm filler. Each placement is an earned, editorial mention of LendForAll on a real website with a real audience:

  • A genuine site with domain authority in the DR 30–60 range and 1,000+ monthly visitors
  • Query-relevant content — Canadian finance, lending, and personal-money topics — not a random blog
  • Written as a real article or review that links to your highest-value loan pages
  • Aimed surgically at the target terms above, strongest-first
The cost comparison that matters: bad-credit and personal-loan keywords are among the most expensive in Canadian paid search — clicks routinely run $10+ each. The ~250 extra organic clicks a month this targets would cost well over $2,500/mo to buy on Google Ads, every month. Authority builds that traffic as an asset you keep.
