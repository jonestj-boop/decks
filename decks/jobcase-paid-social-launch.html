The read

What day one is telling us

✓ What's working

  • The creative is landing. A 7.4% link CTR runs several times the usual Meta benchmark. The video hooks are earning the click.
  • Trials are cheap. $1.27 per Website Start Trial on cold traffic, with two campaigns under $1.05.
  • No fatigue yet. 1.10 frequency means we're reaching fresh people rather than repeating the same audience, so there's plenty of headroom to scale.
  • Every campaign is converting. All three are producing trials on day one, so we're not leaning on a single winner.

! What we're managing

  • Small sample. This is roughly one day and $86 of spend, early enough that we let it run before making calls.
  • Veterans Affairs efficiency. $2.83 per trial is about 2× the others despite a healthy 7.3% CTR. The gap is on the conversion side, not the click.
  • Budget headroom. Spend is well under the combined daily budget, so volume should climb as delivery ramps.
The plan

What we're watching next

The early efficiency earns the right to scale carefully. Here's where our attention goes over the next stretch.

01

Let it stabilize

Hold budgets steady through a full learning window so cost-per-trial settles on real volume before we judge winners.

02

Lean into efficiency

Shift budget toward State Govs and General Motors as they hold their sub-$1.05 trial cost at higher spend.

03

Diagnose Veterans Affairs

Strong clicks, softer conversion. We'll review the post-click experience and offer match before deciding to iterate or cut.
