The read

What the first two days tell us

✓ What's working

  • Cheap trials are holding. $1.32 per Website Start Trial across two days and 119 trials, all on cold traffic.
  • Veterans Affairs turned around. Cost per trial dropped from $2.83 to $1.56 on day two, with more trials (10 to 14). The early laggard is now competitive.
  • General Motors is the steady engine. Around $1.05 both days and the most trials of any campaign each day.
  • The creative earns the click. Day-one link CTR of 7.4% ran several times the usual Meta benchmark.
  • Audiences still fresh. Frequency stayed between 1.10 and 1.21, so we're reaching new people rather than repeating.

! What we're managing

  • State Govs, Florida slipped. Cost per trial rose from $0.98 to $1.92 and trials fell from 23 to 11. We'll watch a third day before deciding whether it's daily noise or a real drop.
  • Day-two volume dipped. Trials went from 68 to 51, mostly from that State Govs pullback, while the other two held.
  • Still a small sample. Two days and about $157 of spend, so these remain early signals, not settled benchmarks.
The plan

What we're watching next

The early efficiency earns the right to scale carefully. Here's where our attention goes over the next stretch.

01

Let it stabilize

Hold budgets through a full learning window so cost per trial settles on real volume before we pick winners.

02

Back the steady performers

Give General Motors and Veterans Affairs room to scale while they hold near or below the blended cost per trial.

03

Watch State Govs, Florida

If the higher cost holds a third day, review the audience and creative before adding budget.
