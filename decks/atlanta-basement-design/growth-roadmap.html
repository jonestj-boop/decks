What we deliberately excluded

Raw research returned 3,414 keywords worth 1.9 million monthly searches, and the raw competitor gap claimed 85,710. After filtering to what you actually sell, the real gap is 16,670, about 19 percent of the headline number. We cut 2,255 keywords worth 1,615,070 searches:

  • Not a basement term: 1,583 keywords, 1,249,930 monthly searches
  • Water / moisture - capped to the planned pre-remodel set only: 366 keywords, 248,290 monthly searches
  • Wrong meaning or product-shopping intent: 134 keywords, 64,190 monthly searches
  • Service on the do-NOT-claim list (epoxy, foundation, structural repair): 34 keywords, 19,490 monthly searches
  • House-plan / real-estate intent, not remodeling: 44 keywords, 12,830 monthly searches
Waterproofing is the hardest call on this list. It is the biggest on-brand cluster we found, roughly 248,000 monthly searches. You do not sell it as a standalone service, and your own warranty page treats it as part of a finishing job. Chasing it would bring you people with water in the basement tonight who want a waterproofing company. We kept only a handful of “fix this before you finish” articles, which feed the work you do sell.
The plan is a decision, not a menu. Alongside it sits a bench of 32 curated next-best targets in your workbook. Each month we re-run the plan against live data: some articles merge, some get reprioritized, a few are replaced from the bench. Every swap comes with a one-line reason. That is the plan working, not the plan failing.
