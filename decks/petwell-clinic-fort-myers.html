Questions for today

Five answers that shape the plan.

PetWellClinic is a franchise brand, so a few things depend on what you control locally and what corporate controls.

01 · Access

Who manages the Google Business Profile and the Fort Myers location page: you, or corporate? Can we add service pages and articles to the site, or do they go through brand approval?

02 · Other clinics nearby

Are there other PetWellClinic locations in Lee County? We want to set radiuses so your keywords don't compete with a sister clinic.

03 · Value of a client

What's a typical first visit worth, and how often does a client come back in a year? That sets the break-even for every package.

04 · Capacity and reviews

How many more walk-ins a day can the team handle? How many Google reviews do you have today, and does anyone ask for them now?

05 · Your best service. Which visits do you most want more of: wellness packages, vaccines, puppy and kitten care, or sick visits? The keyword list should follow what makes the clinic money.
