GAPS IDENTIFIED
Three high-value keyword clusters are sitting dark.
The account has well-built keyword sets and ad copy for Software, Branded, and Competitor searches, but none are actively capturing demand.
1
Software keywords are paused
Will Software, Trust Software, and Estate Planning Software ad groups are all paused in both national campaigns. Zero impressions. These target users searching for the exact type of product FeedingBird is: "estate planning software," "will maker software." This is the highest-intent cluster in the account.
2
No branded campaign exists
No one is bidding on "FeedingBird," "feedingbird.com," or brand variations. At $40K+/month in spend generating 1,200+ sign-ups, brand search volume exists. Without a branded campaign, competitors like LegalZoom or Trust and Will can appear above FeedingBird for its own name.
3
PMax creative lacks differentiation
Both PMax asset groups (Will and Trust) share identical images ("general-lead-only"). Search themes show leakage into irrelevant territory: "affordable living trust attorney near me" and "trust account requirements." Without unique creative per product, Google treats both asset groups as interchangeable.
FOCUS
Additionally, the Atlanta metro campaign has an $875/day budget but has only spent $1,800 total. The geo + exact match combination limits volume. This warrants a conversation with the client about the expansion strategy.

WHAT WE WOULD DO
Five moves to shift the account closer to revenue.
The account is well-built. The opportunity is in filling gaps, moving the optimization target downstream, and reallocating spend from underperformers to proven converters.
1
Shift the bidding target downstream
Move assessment_completed to primary status so Smart Bidding optimizes for users who engage with the product, not just register. This is the single highest-leverage change. We would validate the rationale behind the current setup with the client first.
2
Reactivate Software keywords
Pull all Software and App keywords into a dedicated campaign with its own budget ($50-75/day). The negative keyword work is already built. These are users specifically looking for a tool like FeedingBird.
3
Launch a branded Search campaign
Defend the brand name with a small-budget ($10-15/day) branded campaign. Low CPCs, high conversion rates, and prevents competitors from capturing FeedingBird's own search demand.
4
Restructure PMax creative
Give each asset group (Will and Trust) unique images, add search themes to guide matching, and turn off URL expansion. Block the attorney/financial trust leakage the search theme data is showing.
5
Trim underperformers, reallocate budget
Pause or restructure House and Property ($316 CPA), evaluate Living Will and POA ($123 CPA), and pause Demand Gen retargeting ($725 spent, 0 conversions). Redirect freed budget to DIY and Online ad groups.
+
Future test: Competitor campaign
Once the restructure is in place and performing, test a competitor-targeting campaign (Trust and Will, LegalZoom, Rocket Lawyer). FeedingBird's pricing and one-time payment model are strong differentiators. This is a Phase 2 play.
PRINCIPLE
Same or slightly higher total spend, allocated toward higher-intent searches and optimized for deeper funnel actions. The structure stays clean; we fill the gaps and stop paying for sign-ups that never convert.

THE PLAN
A three-phase path from measurement to scale.
WEEK 1
Fix the signal
Enable advanced matching, complete the Purchase event setup, confirm the pixel fires on every live landing page, consolidate the six flagged ad sets into one, and connect a CRM, whether HubSpot or another system, so qualified conversions flow back to Meta.
DAY 30
Rebalance and expand

Build for scale
