The model

Rent the traffic, learn what converts, then own it.

Paid gets you data in weeks. Content takes months to rank, but then it's yours. Rent to own uses the first to aim the second, so you only build content around searches that have already produced sign-ups.

TikTok is where students spend their time, Meta reaches both students and parents, and Google catches anyone actively searching. All three can use your sign-up list on day one. Anything beyond these three would spread $5,500 too thin to read.

How the split moves

  • Starting split only. From week 6, budget follows cost per paid user, not this table
  • Until employers launch in week 5, their $500 goes to students
  • Each audience gets at least $1,000 in a channel before we judge it there, so nobody makes a call off a handful of clicks
  • TikTok is students only, at $50 a day, which is TikTok's campaign minimum. Spark Ads let us boost videos your ambassadors and founders post, which usually beat polished ads there
  • Reddit ads and LinkedIn wait until we know which audience wins
Your list does the heavy lifting. Uploading the 15,000 sign-ups (hashed, through Google Customer Match, Meta Custom Audiences, and TikTok Custom Audiences) means the platforms start from real RAIN users, not weeks of guessing.
