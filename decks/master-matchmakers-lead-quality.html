The pivot
  1. Point everything at New York and LA. Push your rankings up where the demand already sits, 20,000 impressions a quarter waiting, instead of adding pages in states that bring volume but not your clients.
  2. Trade comparison content for buyer-intent content. "Hire a matchmaker in Manhattan," cost and process pages that pull people ready to hire you, not people weighing you against others.
  3. Route intent traffic to conversion, and qualify it. Move more market-page visitors into pricing and get-started, with a qualifying step so low-fit inquiries never reach your pipeline as bad leads.
  4. Change the scoreboard to qualified leads, not clicks. We tie the content to lead quality and read it within a cycle, so "more spend" is measured against real business, not traffic.
