  • Point authoritative backlinks at the ~1,072 keywords sitting on pages 3–5
  • Prioritize near-miss pages: PC Financial (pos 14), 580–649 Credit Cards (pos 39), CPP Payment Dates (pos 38)

📈 Ongoing SEO + content
