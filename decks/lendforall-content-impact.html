Growth Recommendations

Turning impressions into clicks

🔗 Surgical link building

  • Point authoritative backlinks at the ~2,051 keywords sitting on pages 3–5
  • Prioritize near-miss pages: Best Buy Financing (pos 17), Credit Repair Companies (pos 34), CPP Payment Dates (pos 38), 580–649 Credit Cards (pos 39)
  • Moving a page from position 20 → 8 is where clicks compound

📈 Ongoing SEO + content

  • Keep expanding the cluster around bad-credit lending, local guides & benefits
  • Internally link the 75 articles into the money pages (loan / card applications)
  • Refresh titles & meta on high-impression, low-CTR posts to lift click-through
This report captures the foundation phase. Impressions and rankings are the leading indicators — they're up sharply from zero across 67 of the 75 articles. Clicks are the lagging indicator and follow as rankings mature and links land. The program is tracking exactly where a healthy content build should be at this stage.
