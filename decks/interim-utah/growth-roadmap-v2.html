What we deliberately excluded

Anyone can export a keyword list. The value is in what gets cut.

  • Everything already covered by your 158 live posts. Zero overlap with phase one.
  • Disease-information searches (drug lists, symptoms) where the reader wants medical facts, not a caregiver.
  • Financial-aid and charity searches. You are a care provider, not a benefits office.
  • Insurance-product shopping. You do not sell insurance.
  • Anything outside your Wasatch Front service area.
How each article is built: entity-rich, human-edited, SEO-validated, and checked against your live site and your 158 published posts before it ships, so your guarantee, your rates, and your VA language are stated exactly the way you state them.
