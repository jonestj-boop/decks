Why one channel isn't enough

Meta interrupts merchants. Nobody is there when they go looking.

A merchant who saw your ad, or just got a dispute email, goes to Google or ChatGPT next. Today both send them somewhere else.

Google Search · paid

  • Safe isn't bidding on any of them, including its own name

The brand search leaks

  • "safe app" got 4,381 impressions in 90 days at position 4.6, mostly people after a messaging or safety app
  • A merchant who saw your Meta ad and searches "safe app" has to dig past them
  • 421 reason code searches show safe.app around position 23, with 0 clicks
AI answers

Your selling points, credited to others

  • "Only charge if you win" returned 7 providers, two at 15%. Safe wasn't one
  • "Done-for-you team for Stripe disputes" returned 4 providers. Safe wasn't one
  • robots.txt asks AI tools not to use safe.app in answers (ai-input=no)
What that costs Meta: every merchant who checks you out after seeing an ad and finds nothing, or finds a competitor, is a booked call Meta paid to start and someone else finished. Fixing search and AI makes the Meta budget you already spend convert better.
The integrated model

Three channels, three jobs, one scoreboard.

Each channel does what it's best at. Meta brings volume. Google brings intent and quality. Organic and AI build trust and fill the retargeting pool. All three report to the same CRM, the same events and the same number: cost per sales-qualified call.

Meta · create demand

Volume

Reaches merchants before they search, with proof and fraud hooks.
  • Always-on website booking campaign (quiz)
  • Conditional-logic instant form, same qualifiers
  • Small creative sandbox, 4–6 new statics every two weeks
  • Retargets search and content visitors with booking ads
Google · capture intent

  • Brand campaign to protect "Safe App" searches
  • Platform and category searches (Shopify, Stripe, management services)
  • Switcher searches (Chargeflow alternatives)
  • Same quiz, with Shopify, Stripe and switcher versions
