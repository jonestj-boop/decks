The hard part

A new brand in a new category needs more than ads.

Meta can find buyers, but only once we know who they are and what makes them buy. Each 90-day sprint in this plan answers one question before the next sprint starts.

01 · New category

Nobody's searching for it yet

People don't search for "luxury jacket with a locking pocket." They search for the problem it solves: pickpockets in Vegas, carrying chips safely, theft while traveling. Early demand has to come from ads and creators who show the pocket in use.

02 · No data

Every channel starts at zero

No list to email and no pixel history for Meta to learn from. The first ad dollars do double duty: they build the audience and teach us which audience, message and garment sell.

03 · Q4 timing

Launching in the most expensive quarter

Midterm election ads, Black Friday and holiday retail push Meta costs up in November and December. The heaviest spend goes in January, when ads get cheaper and we have launch data to work from.

The go-to-market strategy

Sell the problem first. The garment is the answer.

Positioning

Luxury menswear that protects what you carry. Looks like Peter Millar, works like a safe. Built for people who carry more than most.

Who we sell to first

High rollers in high-limit rooms carrying chips, tickets and cash. Luxury travelers carrying passports, phones and wallets through airports. We test both and put the budget behind the audience that buys cheaper.

Offer

Full price with no discounts, not even on Black Friday. Waitlist members get early access. The polo (~$200) gets people in, and the jacket and pant bring them back. Gift-ready for the holidays.

Sprint 1 · Waitlist strategyLive mid-October → early access Dec 1

Give people a reason to join before they can buy.

A "notify me" box won't fill a list for a brand nobody's heard of. The waitlist gets a name, a real benefit and a short quiz, so every signup tells us who they are and what they'll buy.

The offer · Why join

The Founders' List

  • Two weeks' head start: buy from Dec 1, before the public launch on Dec 14
  • First pick of sizes from a first run of 2,400 units
  • First to ship, ahead of holiday shipping cutoffs
  • Refer 3 friends, shop 24 hours sooner: a referral link that moves people up the list
  • No discount. Getting in first is the perk, and that keeps full-price positioning intact
The page · 30 seconds

A four-question quiz

  1. Where do you need it? Casino, travel, or both
  2. What do you carry? Chips and cash, passport and phone, or all of it
  3. Which piece first? Polo, half-zip, full-zip or pant
  4. Your size, so we know demand by size before launch

Then email, plus an optional SMS opt-in with a proper consent checkbox. Answers tag each subscriber so the emails and ads they see match.

Where signups come from

Four sources, one page

  • Meta lead ads, $6K: casino vs. travel creative, showing the pocket in use
  • Sarasani: teaser posts and stories with a link to the list
  • Your network, "Founding 100": personal invites to athletes and high rollers, who get access on Nov 30, a day before everyone else
  • Organic social + referrals: every signup gets a link to share

Sprint 1 · Waitlist strategySignup → sale

Keep them warm for six weeks, then give them a deadline.

Someone who signs up in October won't buy on Dec 1 unless they've heard from Fortin in between. The sequence explains the product before we ask for the sale, and each subscriber gets the casino or travel version.

Built once, used all year: this sequence becomes Fortin's permanent welcome flow. Anyone who signs up after launch gets the same story, ending with a first-purchase nudge instead of early access.
What we'll watch: cost per signup and quiz completion each week (Meta budget follows the cheaper audience), open and click rates on each email, and the early-access conversion rate. We're planning on 3–5% of the list buying in the first two weeks.

Everyone who joins and doesn't buy by Dec 14 goes into a Meta retargeting audience for launch. The quiz's size answers help plan the ad budget around the sizes with the most stock.
