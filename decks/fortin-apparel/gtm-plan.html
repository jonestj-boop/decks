The hard part

Ads alone can't reach a high roller. People and places can.

Meta can't target "plays in the high-limit room." That audience is reached through the people they trust and the places they go. So this plan pairs paid ads with a creator program and a casino channel from day one.

01 · New category

Nobody searches for it yet

People search for the problem, not the product: carrying chips safely, pickpockets in Vegas. Demand starts with creators and ads that show the pocket in use. Search comes later, once we can measure it.

02 · No data

Every channel starts at zero

No list, pixel history or reviews. The first dollars in every channel buy information as much as sales, so each sprint is set up to answer one question.

03 · 2,400 units

Ads alone would take years

At a realistic conversion rate, Meta by itself sells about 56 garments a month. Selling through the first run needs several channels working together, not just a bigger ad budget.

Win Vegas first, then take what works to travelers.

The first market

Win one market before you try for two.

$100K split across casino and travel from day one would mean half a test in each. Starting in the casino puts the whole budget where Fortin has the strongest story and the most help.

Why casino first

Where Fortin wins

  • Nobody else serves it. Casino players have no luxury apparel with a secure pocket.
  • The help is already there: Sarasani plays blackjack, and your network is in Vegas.
  • It's in one place. Casinos, hosts, boutiques and events are all within a few miles, so the offline channel can actually be run.
  • The pocket demo sells itself: chips, tickets and cash in a jacket at the table.
Why travel waits

Bigger market, more competition

  • Anti-theft travel clothing already exists: pickpocket-proof pants and travel vests with hidden pockets
  • Fortin's edge there is style, which is harder to show in an ad than a pocket at a blackjack table
  • We still collect travel interest in the waitlist quiz, then test it properly in Sprint 3 with real creative and data
Not casino only: casino comes first. If the Sprint 3 travel test comes in at a lower cost per customer, the budget follows it.

Sprint 1 · Waitlist strategyLive mid-October → early access Dec 1

Give people a reason to join before they can buy.

A "notify me" box won't fill a list for a brand nobody's heard of. The waitlist gets a name, a real benefit and a short quiz, so every signup tells us who they are and what they'll buy.

The offer · Why join

The Founders' List

  • Two weeks' head start: buy from Dec 1, before the public launch on Dec 14
  • First pick of sizes from a first run of 2,400 units
  • First to ship, ahead of holiday shipping cutoffs
  • Refer 3 friends, shop 24 hours sooner: a referral link that moves people up the list
  • No discount. Getting in first is the perk, and that keeps full-price positioning intact
The page · 30 seconds

A four-question quiz

  1. What's your game? Blackjack, poker, slots, roulette, or "I travel more than I play"
  2. What do you carry? Chips and cash, passport and phone, or all of it
  3. Which piece first? Polo, half-zip, full-zip or pant
  4. Your size, so we know demand by size before launch

Then email, plus an optional SMS opt-in with a proper consent checkbox. The travel answers size up the Sprint 3 test before we spend anything on it.

  • Your network, "Founding 100": personal invites to athletes and high rollers, who get access on Nov 30, a day before everyone else
  • Referrals: every signup gets a link to share

Channel · Creators

Build a small bench of gambling creators, paid mostly from sales.

One big creator gives you a launch spike. A bench of smaller ones, each with a trackable code, shows you who actually sells, and their posts become the ads that perform best for a new brand.

Who

Sarasani plus a bench of 4–6

  • Anchor: John Sarasani, on the terms of your deal
  • Bench: 4–6 blackjack, poker and high-limit slots creators with engaged followings in the 10K–100K range
  • Picked for audience fit and engagement, not follower count
How they're paid

Product, a small fee, then commission

  • A garment to wear on camera
  • A small flat fee per post from the creator fund
  • An affiliate code with commission on every sale, paid from revenue rather than the budget
  • Usage rights so we can run their posts as ads
Who does what

Your relationships, our ads

  • You: recruit and manage the creators. Brand partnerships are your background.
  • EmberTribe: briefs, tracking codes, and running their posts as Meta partnership ads
  • Monthly scorecard: sales by code and cost per customer from their ads
Why partnership ads matter: Meta lets a brand run a creator's post as an ad from the creator's own handle. For a brand nobody knows yet, an ad from a player people already follow usually beats one from Fortin's own account. We run these from Sprint 1.
