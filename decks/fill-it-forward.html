CEO Thought-Leadership Program

Matt's constraint isn't confidence — it's time. We design around that.

A batch-production workflow that turns one recording session into a quarter's worth of content, without asking Matt to be "always on."

How a session works

  • Pre-work (us): we build a scripted talking-point outline from Matt's conference material and Reuse 365 data — no blank-page problem, no "be salesy" pressure
  • Recording (Matt, remote): one focused session, webinar-style — consistent with how he already presents
  • Post-production (us): clipped into LinkedIn video, quote graphics, and ghostwritten long-form articles
  • Publishing (us): scheduled into the LinkedIn content calendar alongside the impact-data and event-case-study pillars
Why this solves your stated blocker: you told us finding time to film is the real obstacle, not Matt's ability on camera. A single scripted, remote, batch session removes the scheduling friction entirely.
Tone match: Matt's own words — "reuse is simple, reuse at scale" — become the throughline, not a generic thought-leadership template. Value-first, not promotional.
