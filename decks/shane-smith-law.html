How the map pack works

The map ranks offices, not websites. Your three offices set the playing field.

Google picks the three firms by relevance (does your profile match the search), distance (how close your office is to the searcher), and prominence (reviews, links, citations, and site authority). You can't move an office, so we win on the other two.
