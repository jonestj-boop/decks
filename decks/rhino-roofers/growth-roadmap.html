  • Other Texas metros. Storm keywords pull in Houston, Dallas and College Station volume. Large numbers, zero relevance to your service area. Removed.

  • Pure DIY queries. Someone searching how to patch a roof themselves is not going to call a contractor.
  • More city pages. You have 284. Adding blog versions of them would split your own rankings.
