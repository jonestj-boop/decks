## P2 — should fix

- **[B] Slide 10 — "Video already earns nearly 2x the ROAS of static images on Meta (0.94 vs. 0.57)."** The math: 0.94 / 0.57 = 1.65x. That's roughly 65% higher, not "nearly 2x" (which implies ~90–100% higher). The two underlying numbers are correct and trace to the Meta Ads Audit PDF's Creative Format table (Video ROAS 0.94, Image/static ROAS 0.57), but the characterization inflates the gap. In a room with a client who's watching every number closely, a magnitude claim that doesn't hold up to quick mental math is exactly the kind of thing that erodes trust even when the underlying data is right. Fix: "roughly 1.6x" or "65% higher," or drop the multiplier and just cite the two ROAS figures.

- **[B] Slide 9 — "Shopping + Search on your highest-intent, already-proven verticals (A250, Army, Navy)."** Of the three, only A250 has Meta campaign-level data in the audit confirming strong performance (A250 collection - V2 at 1.69 ROAS, A250 collection - funnel at 1.36, VENTAS | A250 collection - Shopify at 1.29). The audit's campaign-level table does not include Army or Navy campaigns at all — the one military-adjacent line item it does show, AIRFORCE v2, actually underperformed at 0.49 ROAS. Grouping Army and Navy with A250 under "already-proven" asserts a level of evidence the provided audit doesn't contain for those two verticals specifically. This directly feeds a BOFU budget-allocation recommendation, so if asked "show me the Army/Navy numbers," there isn't audit data to back it up. Fix: either soften to "your strongest licensed verticals" (which the transcript does support — these are core, well-established product lines) or scope the "proven" claim to A250 alone and frame Army/Navy as high-confidence-but-unaudited-on-Meta.
