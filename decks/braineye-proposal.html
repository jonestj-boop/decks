Quality management

Audit-ready by design.

How the site meets 21 CFR 11.10 without the ten-click process you have today.

Quality management · 21 CFR 11.10

Edit, submit, approve, approve. Then it's live.

Step 1
EditEditor changes a draft version
Step 2
SubmitGoes to review with a visual diff
Step 3
ApproveFirst approver signs, or rejects with a comment
Step 4
ApproveA different approver signs. Never the author
Automatic
LiveRevision number, footer and audit entry update

Immutability

Published versions can't be edited or deleted, even by a Super Admin. Removals need two approvals and stay archived.

Audit trail

Who, when, which version and a visual diff, chained so tampering shows, copied nightly to write-once AWS storage. Exports to CSV or PDF.

Dual approval

Two different approvers, never the author, for every publish, edit or delete across all content types.

Approvals as signatures

Password re-entry, with name, date, time and meaning recorded. Also covers Part 11 sections 11.50, 11.70 and 11.200.

Version footer

Every page shows "Revision 3, March 2027" style footers automatically.

Why custom

No off-the-shelf workflow plugin enforces two non-author approvers or protects the log from admins. A small plugin is also easier to validate.

How it holds up in an audit

Every 11.10 clause, mapped.

11.10RequirementHow we meet it
(a)ValidationValidation pack (requirements, risk assessment, IQ/OQ/PQ scripts, traceability matrix), run with your QA team
(b)Accurate, complete copiesAny version and the full audit log export to readable PDF and CSV
(c)Record protectionLocked versions, a chained log copied nightly to write-once storage, retention per your SOP
(d)Limited accessNamed accounts, three roles, multi-factor sign-in, login rate limiting
(e)Audit trailAutomatic, time-stamped log of who, what, when and the diff. Never overwrites earlier entries
(f)Operational checksDraft, review 1, review 2, publish is enforced. Steps can't be skipped
(g)Authority checksPermissions checked on every action. Authors can't approve their own change
(h)Device checksProtected admin sign-in with MFA. Optional IP restriction
(i)TrainingAdmin guide, recorded session and a training record template
(j)Signature accountabilityA draft update to your website change-control SOP, owned by BrainEye
(k)Documentation controlsVersion-controlled docs. Code moves dev, staging, production with a change log

Forms, Zendesk and flipbooks

Every enquiry lands in the right Zendesk queue.

Zendesk forms

  • Support, General and Media forms each create a ticket tagged by form type.
  • Direct to the Zendesk API with no third-party connector, with automatic retries. WordPress keeps no copy once Zendesk confirms.
  • Tickets raised in your existing Zendesk, so customers follow them at support.braineye.com.
  • Cloudflare Turnstile spam protection and a required consent checkbox.

Onboarding flipbooks

  • One master written in WordPress as structured pages.
  • Automatic translation with WPML and DeepL, plus a language switcher.
  • Page-turning viewer on desktop and mobile, with a PDF download per language.
  • Same QMS rules for the master and each translation. We recommend one fluent approver per language.
  • Hidden from search by default, and ready to move behind the portal login.

Priced for up to about 40 pages and three languages besides English.

SEO, analytics and security

Cookieless analytics, tight security.

SEO and indexing

  • A "Hide from search" toggle per page: noindex, out of the sitemap and site search.
  • New posts go into the sitemap with canonical tags and structured data, and are pushed to Bing and other IndexNow engines.
  • Editable meta and social cards, clean URLs, 301 redirects from every changed URL.

Analytics

We recommend Plausible: EU-hosted and cookieless, so no consent prompt is needed for it.

Our GTM advice: remove it. It loads tracking that needs consent and lets scripts bypass your QMS approvals. We can add it back behind consent if you run paid campaigns.

Security and hosting

  • Managed WordPress hosting with development, staging and production.
  • Cloudflare firewall, bot protection, rate limiting and security headers.
  • SSL, multi-factor sign-in for every account, role-based access.
  • Daily backups with a restore test, malware scanning, uptime monitoring.
  • Checked against your current measures under NDA, matching or exceeding each.
