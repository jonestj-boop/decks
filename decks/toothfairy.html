What We Found First

Three Gaps, Live Right Now

None of these showed up on the discovery call because they weren't visible without account access. All three are fixable in days, not months.

Urgent

Analytics Blind Spot

  • toothfairyapp.co.uk now 301-redirects to www.toothfairyapp.co.uk — confirmed live today.
  • GA4 sessions ran ~430/day through July, then fell fast once the redirect went live — 33/day on Aug 3, down to 1–3/day by Aug 6, still there today.
  • GSC shows the opposite over the same window: daily impressions on www climbed from ~20 to 2,000–3,600.
Read: not a traffic drop. A tracking tag that never followed the domain to its new home.
Ongoing

Conversion Signal Gap

  • 75 form_submit events logged directly in GA4 over the last 90 days, roughly 25 a month of real activity.
  • Not one of them is marked as a GA4 key event (conversion).
  • Anything that leans on GA4 conversions, including Google Ads Smart Bidding, currently has zero signal to work with.
Read: a fast fix once flagged — needs to happen before any spend leans on GA4 conversions.
Urgent

Broken Content Pages

  • No sitemap.xml exists (404) — Google has no roadmap of your pages to (re)crawl post-migration.
  • Your own /knowledge hub index redirects to a URL that 404s — a dead end for anyone, or any crawler, trying to browse your blog.
  • 8 of 19 known content URLs we spot-checked 404 outright, including the retainer-cleaner article (was ranking #6, 8,100 searches/mo) and /contact-us/.
Read: this explains the "best at home teeth whitening kits" ranking that vanished between our two data pulls this week — that exact page 404s right now.
