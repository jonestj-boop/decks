What We Found First

Two Measurement Gaps, Live Right Now

Neither of these showed up on the discovery call because they weren't visible without account access. Both are fixable in days, not months.

Urgent

Analytics Blind Spot

  • toothfairyapp.co.uk now 301-redirects to www.toothfairyapp.co.uk — confirmed live today.
  • GA4 sessions on the tracked property ran ~430/day through July, then fell fast once the redirect went live — 33/day on Aug 3, down to 1–3/day by Aug 6, and still there today.
  • Search Console shows the opposite story over the same window: daily impressions on the www property climbed from ~20 to 2,000–3,600 over that same week.
Read: this isn't a traffic drop. It's a tracking tag that never followed the domain to its new home. Every day it stays broken is a day of conversion, funnel, and channel data you can't get back.
Ongoing

Conversion Signal Gap

  • 75 form_submit events logged directly in GA4 over the last 90 days, roughly 25 a month of real activity.
  • Not one of them is marked as a GA4 key event (conversion).
  • Anything that leans on GA4 conversions for optimization, including Google Ads Smart Bidding if you ever turn it on, currently has zero signal to work with.
Read: this kind of gap tends to go unnoticed for a long time because everything else looks normal. It's a fast fix once flagged, and it needs to happen before any spend leans on GA4 conversions for optimization.
