What's working

  • Repeat buyers. In a typical month, 60 to 70% of online orders come from returning customers
  • Order size. About $57 an order, and some months closer to $80
  • Yearly value. Around $150 to $200 a year per customer across the mix. Your best buyers order every two weeks and spend $1,000 or more

Part two · Keep them coming back

Subscribe and email.

Ads pay for the first bag. Subscriptions and email bring in every bag after it, without paying for the customer again.

Subscribe and save

Start with the buyers you already have.

Most of your online orders already come from repeat customers. Give them a subscribe button and a reason to use it, and some of your revenue becomes predictable before we spend anything on ads.

Weeks 1–2 · Set up

Turn it on

  • Subscription app on Shopify with delivery every 2, 3, or 4 weeks
  • Subscriber discount and perks, set by you (for example 10% off and free shipping)
  • Subscribe option shown first on every coffee page
  • Easy skip, swap, and pause, so people don't cancel
Weeks 2–4 · Existing buyers

Invite your regulars

  • Email everyone who has ordered twice or more
  • Personal note from you on why subscribing helps farmers plan too
  • Follow-up to anyone who reorders by hand
Ongoing · New buyers

Convert first orders

  • Subscribe offer in the post-purchase emails
  • Reminder about 3 weeks after a 12oz order, when the bag runs low
  • Ads retargeting one-time buyers with the subscribe offer
We've done this for a coffee brand. They had a lot of existing customers on subscription. Every dollar added to the average order raises what we can afford to pay for a customer. These are your decisions to make. We'll model each one against your margins first.
