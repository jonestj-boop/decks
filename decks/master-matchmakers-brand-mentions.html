The Play

Brand Mentions — how it works

Each "brand mention" is a positive, third-party article that names Master Matchmakers the #1 choice — engineered to feed both AI answers and Google.

📰

1 · Publish the placement

  • A listicle / review article naming you the #1 brand for a target keyword or angle
  • Placed on quality, real websites the AI already trusts
  • Written to be quoted — the positive, on-brand source

2 · Anchor it to your site

  • Link to each placement from a dedicated reviews page on your domain
  • Stack genuine client reviews there too
  • Consolidates the signal under your brand

3 · Amplify with links

  • Build links pointing at those articles to grow their authority

  • Two-pronged: lifts AI answers and traditional rankings
