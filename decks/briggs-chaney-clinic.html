Three Channels, Three Different Stories

Google Ads has real budget and real activity behind it, but the measurement underneath it is broken. SEO has essentially never been invested in. Local SEO is a tale of two locations — one with years of trust built up, one starting from zero.

What's Working

  • Silver Spring's Google Business Profile: 4.5★ across 744 reviews — a genuine trust asset in a competitive urgent-care market
