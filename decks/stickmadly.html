The account duplicates by vertical instead of consolidating. Six separate campaigns run broad prospecting. FC Barcelona alone has six campaigns for one product line. Retargeting is split across four overlapping structures.

The account is paying for its own learning phase, over and over. A Meta ad set needs roughly 50 optimization events within 7 days to exit the learning phase.
