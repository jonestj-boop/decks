Who we're talking to

Women 35–55, in the thick of a hormonal shift

She's noticing weight that won't move the way it used to, energy that disappears by 3pm, sleep that isn't restful, and she's been told "that's just aging." She wants a plan that's actually built for her body, from someone she trusts before she ever sits in the chair. Every script below is written to speak to her directly — informative and reassuring, never clinical or salesy.

Topic Pillars

Five pillars, weighted toward what converts

Hormone Reset and The Plan (medical weight loss) anchor the week — they're the two services with the most search intent and the most misinformation to correct. Real Vitals and Meet Your Team build the trust that turns a follower into a first booking. Full Spectrum rotates in the rest of the menu.

Before recording

Two ground rules

Consent first. Any patient face, voice, before/after, or identifying detail needs a signed release on file before it's filmed or posted — including Stories. When in doubt, anonymize (voiceover, quote card, blurred) instead of skipping the win.

No individual medical claims. Frame outcomes as "in my experience" or "for the right patient," never as a guarantee. Every post below is written that way — keep that framing if you improvise on camera.
