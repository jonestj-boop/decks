Fix first

Site and catalog fixes before launch

About 6.5% of shoppers who land on a product page add to cart, at any budget, and only about 18% of started checkouts finish. More ad spend won't move those numbers. These fixes will:

  • Sold-out items out of ads, automatically. Stock status syncs from Shopify to Meta and Google Merchant Center, so sold-out items drop out of ads and come back when restocked. No more deleting products in Shopify.
  • Ads only for your four essential collections: 14K band rings, hoop earrings, bangle bracelets and chains (28 products), with titles that name the metal, changed in the ad feed only and tried on band rings first.
  • "Notify me when back in stock" on sold-out items, so a sold-out visit becomes a lead instead of a lost sale.
  • Real product reviews. A reviews app that asks actual customers for a review after delivery, plus your Trustpilot rating on the site. Nothing artificial.
  • A checkout review. We test every payment method (Shop Pay, Affirm, Afterpay, card) on mobile and desktop and fix what's causing the drop-off.
  • Cleaner tracking on the steps before purchase, so Meta can tell which ads start the sales.
