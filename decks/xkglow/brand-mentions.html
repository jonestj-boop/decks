Why it matters

Citations and mentions come from different places

01

Citations come from your own site

When an AI engine needs product details, it pulls them from xkglow.com. The blog program we're running now builds this side.

02

Mentions come from everyone else

When someone asks "best app-controlled underglow kit" or "best rock lights for a Cybertruck," the engine decides who to recommend mostly from third-party roundups, reviews and buyer's guides. Publishing more on your own blog can't put XKGLOW in those articles.

The gap: your content is set up to be cited, but not enough outside sites are recommending XKGLOW. Brand mentions fill that gap.
What we'd do

Editorial placements on real sites

Each month we place XKGLOW in editorial content on established sites. These are listicles, reviews and buyer's guides written for the searches your customers run, not link drops or paid directories.

  • Every placement is on a DR 30 to 60 site with at least 1,000 monthly visitors
  • Each article is relevant to a buyer query, so AI engines have a reason to read it
  • We agree on target topics with your team up front, and after that it adds nothing to your review queue

What to expect

An honest timeline

We can't promise that ChatGPT will name XKGLOW for a specific query. Nobody controls that, and you should be skeptical of anyone who says they do. What we commit to is the placements themselves: how many, the quality of the sites, and the topics.

1

First 30 days

We agree on topics and angles with you, and the first articles go live. We record a baseline of mentions by engine, plus a set of buyer-style prompts we run through ChatGPT, Gemini and Google AI Mode.

2

Days 60 to 90

Placements get indexed and start showing up for those category searches. This is when early movement in mentions usually appears, first in the engines that rely most on third-party content. For XKGLOW, those are ChatGPT and Gemini, where you've lost the most ground.

3

Days 90 to 180

The effect builds as each new placement adds to the ones before it, and the engines start to treat XKGLOW as a brand they recommend in the category.

To be straight about scale: 2 to 3 placements a month is a focused test, not a takeover of the category. After one quarter you'll have 6 to 9 placements live and enough data to see whether mentions are moving. The bigger lift comes in months 4 to 6 if you continue. At 5 to 10 a month, you'd have 15 to 30 placements live by the end of the quarter, which gives the engines far more to draw on.
