Who buys and who you're up against

The buyer is shopping before surgery or right after the first PT visit.

People don't search for "TheraDeck". They search for what they need to get through recovery, or for how to do their home exercises when they can't get on the floor. That's where TheraDeck has to show up.

Three buyers, three moments

BuyerWhen they search
Pre-op patient2–4 weeks before a knee or hip replacement, building a "what to buy" list
Post-op patientAfter the PT hands over a home exercise sheet and the floor isn't an option
Adult child or caregiverBuying for a parent who is aging in place or coming home from rehab
Direct to consumer comes first. Clinics have sold a few, but a busy clinic doesn't have the 10 minutes it takes to walk a patient through the product. The website is where the volume has to come from.

The model · Rent to own

Rent the traffic, learn what sells, then own it.

Paid ads put TheraDeck in front of buyers this month. Content and links take months to rank, but then the traffic is yours and you stop paying for every click. Rent to own uses the first to aim the second, so we only build content around searches that have already sold boards.

Build what ranks

Why this order matters for a $229 product: margins on a single board leave little room for wasted clicks. Paid tells us fast which searches are worth it. Content then takes over those searches at a fraction of the cost per order.

Own · Backlink & brand mention strategy

Replace 1,600 junk links with a few hundred that matter.

Ranking a new site in a health category takes trust. That comes from relevant sites linking to you and from being named in the lists buyers and AI tools read. Here's the order we'd build it in.

StepWhat we doWhyWhen

Why brand mentions first: when someone asks an AI tool "what do I need after knee replacement," it answers from list-style articles. Being named in those lists puts TheraDeck in the answer, alongside walkers and raised toilet seats.
What we won't do: buy PBN links, private blog networks or bulk directory submissions. That's the kind of link already sitting in your profile, and it's why the domain has 1.6K links and no rankings.
