Flushie.com AEO — why we cut the sockpuppet playbook

The reasoning behind the version of the strategy we're actually recommending, and a flag for leadership on the standard offering.

Prepared for: Josh / strategy · September 2026 · Companion to decks/flushie/growth-roadmap.html
TL;DR. The inbound "flushie.com Reddit Proposal" is built around fake accounts posting fabricated testimonials to manufacture consensus for the LLMs. We removed that and rebuilt the strategy on earned presence. This memo says why — and flags that the risky method isn't unique to this proposal; it's written into how we describe AEO placements on the rate card. That's a leadership call worth making on purpose.

What the inbound proposal actually asked for

The research in the PDF is fine — Reddit really is heavily weighted by AI answer engines, the licensing deals and citation-share numbers are real, and the subreddit/query mapping is useful. The problem is the execution method, which is stated plainly:

  • Create threads that "mirror real people users"
  • Use "different accounts" / "other independent reddit accounts" to post brand mentions
  • Write first-person testimonials as if from customers ("I just keep a mini bottle of Flushie in my caddy…")
  • Goal, in their words: make the LLMs "recognize that consensus as factual" — when the consensus is manufactured by paid operators

That is astroturfing: coordinated inauthentic behavior plus fabricated endorsements. It's deceptive by design — the whole point is that neither the Reddit reader nor the model can tell the "customers" are us.

Why it's a bad bet for the client (and for us)

RiskWhat it means here
FTC fake-review ruleThe rule in force since Oct 2024 bans fabricated reviews and testimonials from people who didn't use the product or don't exist. Civil penalties run per violation. A sockpuppet program is the textbook target — and the agency that operates it shares exposure, not just the brand.
Reddit ToSCoordinated inauthentic behavior and vote manipulation get accounts shadowbanned and content purged by mods. Translation: the client pays for "consensus" that gets deleted. It doesn't even reliably persist long enough to be trained on.
DetectabilityManufactured Reddit consensus is exactly the pattern retrieval systems are being tuned to detect and down-weight. We'd be building the client's visibility on the thing the engines are actively working to discount.
Brand blowbackFlushie is a hygiene brand that donates to Water.org and sells on trust. If it's caught faking Reddit reviews, that becomes the story — one screenshot, indefinite shelf life. The downside is wildly asymmetric to the upside.
The honest version is also the durable one. Real citations compound and survive model updates. Fabricated ones are a liability with a countdown on them. We're not choosing ethics over results — for AEO specifically, the ethical version is the one that actually keeps working.

What we're recommending instead

Same objective — Flushie becomes the answer AI engines give — built on presence we earn:

  • Own the citable source. Turn the buyer queries into genuinely useful FAQ/comparison pages on flushie.com, structured for retrieval. Fully in our control, needs no one's permission, highest leverage. This is also where standalone ClusterMagic sells in.
  • Disclosed community participation. A clearly-identified brand presence adding real value where subs allow it, per each community's rules.
  • Earned advocacy. Gifting/sampling to real creators and customers with FTC-compliant disclosure — real people, real mentions.
  • Roundups & PR. Land Flushie in the real "best pre-poo spray" listicles the engines already cite.

The client deck presents this and includes a "what we do / what we won't do" slide that turns the stance into a trust differentiator. For a values-led brand, that's a selling point, not a limitation.

Flag for leadership: the rate card says the quiet part

This isn't only about one inbound proposal. Our own partner-rate-cards.html defines an AEO placement as:

"One Reddit thread interaction (comment on existing thread OR new seeded thread with coordinated responses)" — and the AI Visibility inclusions list "Thread seeding (new posts with coordinated brand responses)."

Read literally, "new posts with coordinated brand responses" describes seeding a thread and answering it from brand-controlled accounts — the same mechanism we're declining here. The existing doctronic-reddit-aeo deck goes further, selling "multiple account profiles to avoid pattern detection," "account profile setup & karma building," and "upvote acceleration."

Recommendation: decide deliberately what an "AEO placement" means, then make the language match. Options, roughly: (a) redefine placements as disclosed participation + owned content + earned advocacy and update the rate card and the doctronic deck to match; (b) keep a seeding component but require disclosure and drop "avoid pattern detection" framing; or (c) accept the current model with eyes open on the FTC/Reddit exposure above. My rec is (a). I did not change the rate card or any other client deck — flagging only.
