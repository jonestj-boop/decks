## Slide 10 · The ClusterMagic engine

It's built on three pillars, and we've run it for four years with zero penalties from Google.

The first pillar is entity architecture. We build topic clusters around subjects Google already understands, with hub pages and supporting articles, so Google sees you as an authority on the topic.

The second is internal linking. Every new article links to the ones before it, so each one adds weight to the whole cluster.

The third is expert sourcing and human review. Every draft cites a real expert, which for you means your guests. A person edits it, and it goes into your CMS for your approval before anything publishes. Every claim traces back to a real source. That's why this approach holds up through Google updates.
