Pitch Angle

Consolidate before you expand. They're not starting from zero. Budget is already split across Meta and Google Display/YouTube. Come in as "let's professionalize and focus what's already running," not "let's build your program from scratch" or "let's add a channel." SEO needs a cleanup phase (disavow spam links, fix zero branded search) before it's a month-one win, so don't propose adding Search/Shopping or SEO spend yet.

Suggested Agenda

  1. Open: reference that you pulled their live Meta ads before the call
  2. Confirm Mario's role and who else touches marketing decisions
  3. Current state: how the $3K–$4K splits between media spend and any help today
  4. What's working: which collections move fastest (Army / Navy / anime / America 250)
  5. Diagnose the scatter: how they decide what to scale vs. kill today
  6. Channel map: confirm the full spread (Meta + Google Display/YouTube today, Search/Shopping untouched)
  7. Next step: propose a Meta account audit as the low-risk first deliverable

Discovery Questions

  • Of the $3–4K, how much is media spend vs. any agency/freelance fees today?
  • Are the military, sports, and anime collections officially licensed, or reproductions?
  • Do you have a CAC or ROAS target you're tracking against?
  • Google Ads run under "Miguel Angel Cubias Samper." Is that you, or someone else on the team?
  • Beyond Meta and Google Display/YouTube, any Search, Shopping, or TikTok spend we haven't seen?
  • Are you evaluating agencies now, or is this exploratory?
