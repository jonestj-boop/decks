Section 1

Launch plan: email first, perk attached

Your customer list is the launch channel. Every migrated customer must activate their new store account by email anyway, so the activation notice and the store announcement arrive as one coordinated moment, with a reason to buy attached.

T−7 days

Teaser to the full list

"Something new is coming to DogiZone": the pre-launch email to all migrated contacts (sets expectations for the activation email, builds the open-rate habit before the ask).

Launch day

Announcement + account activation + Welcome Perk

The big send: new store is live, here's your account (one-click activation; passwords can't migrate, which we turn into a feature: "your new account is ready"), and your Welcome Perk for the first online order.

T+3

Reminder to non-openers + social push

Re-send with a new subject line to anyone who didn't open; same-day posts to the DogiZone Facebook/Instagram audiences; staff mention it at checkout and daycare pickup.

T+10

Perk last call

Final reminder before the launch perk expires. From here the always-on flows (welcome, abandoned cart, replenishment) take over.

Marketing plan: lean start, earned scaling

Final budget & allocation pending EmberTribe Ads team confirmation

You already have an active ads engagement with us (Google + Meta). The launch simply re-aims that program at a store that can finally convert and track properly.

Lean starting level: recommended test budget

ChannelMonthly spendWhat runs
Google$1,200–1,800Google Shopping ads on the new product feed (Google Merchant Center finally points at your own domain), branded search protection, local inventory angles
Meta$800–1,200Retargeting site visitors + customer-list audiences; local prospecting in the Rockville delivery radius
Lean total (ad spend)$2,000–3,000/moPaid directly by DogiZone to platforms, per the retainer terms

Messaging we lead with

  • Same-day local: "Order by 2pm, it's at your door tonight, or pick up in 2 hours." Chewy can't say it.
  • Auto-ship: "Never run out of food again", aimed at the consumables buyer, your most valuable repeat customers.
  • Trust: Best of Bethesda 2026 winner, staff who've trained 30,000+ dogs, a store stocked by its own trainers and groomers.

Scaling triggers: what earns more budget

  • Return on ad spend (revenue per ad dollar) clears the target for 2 consecutive weeks → step budget up ~25–50%
  • A product category shows outsized return → dedicated campaign for it
  • Email + ads together saturate the local audience → evaluate an additional channel (adds $1,000/mo management per the retainer)
Pending: exact budget split, target ROAS threshold, and campaign structure come from the EmberTribe Ads team who run your account; being confirmed now and will be plugged into this page.
