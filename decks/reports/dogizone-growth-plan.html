Section 1

Launch plan — email first, perk attached

Your customer list is the launch channel. Every migrated customer must activate their new store account by email anyway — so the activation notice and the store announcement arrive as one coordinated moment, with a reason to buy attached.

T−7 days

Teaser to the full list

"Something new is coming to DogiZone" — the pre-launch email to all migrated contacts (sets expectations for the activation email, builds the open-rate habit before the ask).

Launch day

Announcement + account activation + Welcome Perk

The big send: new store is live, here's your account (one-click activation — passwords can't migrate, which we turn into a feature: "your new account is ready"), and your Welcome Perk for the first online order.

T+3

Reminder to non-openers + social push

Re-send with a new subject line to anyone who didn't open; same-day posts to the DogiZone Facebook/Instagram audiences; staff mention it at checkout and daycare pickup.

T+10

Perk last-call

Final reminder before the launch perk expires. From here the always-on flows (welcome, abandoned cart, replenishment) take over.

The Welcome Perk — our recommendation

Consistent with the "Welcome Perks, not discounts" positioning you approved for the services site, we recommend leading with a gift, not a markdown:

OptionMechanicWhy / cost shape
Gift with purchase RecommendedFree DogiZone slip lead (your own brand) on first online orders over $75Protects margin, puts your own product in every bag, feels premium not discount-y. Cost ≈ your COGS on the lead per redemption.
Percent off15% off first online order (capped)Simplest to understand; highest redemption; directly discounts margin.
Service tie-inFree DogiWash pass with first order over $50Drives store ↔ services crossover; near-zero hard cost; limited to local customers.
Also in the building: counter signage + QR bag stuffers pointing at the new store, so the ~daily foot traffic feeds online account activations. We'll supply the print-ready file; printing is a client-side cost (minimal).

All emails in this sequence are covered by the Mailchimp flows in the Shopify SOW. The perk choice is the one decision we need from you — cost of redemptions sits with DogiZone.

Messaging we lead with

  • Same-day local: "Order by 2pm, it's at your door tonight — or pick up in 2 hours." Chewy can't say it.
  • Auto-ship: "Never run out of food again" — aimed at the consumables buyer, your highest-LTV segment.

Scaling triggers — what earns more budget

  • A product category shows outsized return → dedicated campaign for it
