Overview

What this covers

Six automated flows, seventeen emails. Together they cover the full customer lifecycle — capture, convert, repeat, recover — and they're the piece that turns the new store from a catalog into a revenue engine.

This maps the automation scope that was left out of the dev migration quote as an optional upgrade. Everything here runs on Mailchimp connected to the new Shopify store. Emails are designed and coded in the DogiZone brand, loaded into Mailchimp as custom templates, and assembled into Customer Journeys.

The stack

How Shopify talks to Mailchimp

The official Mailchimp for Shopify app (free, one-click OAuth install) syncs customers, orders, products, and carts into Mailchimp continuously. That sync is what powers the triggers below — cart abandoned, product purchased, category purchased, time since last order — and ties every email to revenue in reporting.

  • Shopify store → customer, order, product, and cart data → Mailchimp audience
  • eTailPet customer export → one-time import into the same audience, tagged by source
  • Signup capture (site popup + footer + checkout opt-in) → audience with source tags
  • Every flow sends from DogiZone's domain — authenticated with DKIM/SPF during setup
One platform constraint to scope around: Mailchimp's API cannot create Customer Journeys — the journey canvas itself is assembled in the Mailchimp UI. This is a Mailchimp restriction, not a tooling gap, and most ESPs share it. Everything around the journeys is fully scripted (templates, tags, segments, audience). Journey assembly is roughly 30–60 minutes per flow with everything pre-built, and we handle it — it just can't be waved away as "all automated," so it's budgeted honestly below.
Check before kickoff: DogiZone's eTailPet Plus tier bundles a Mailchimp integration — the client may already have a Mailchimp account with an audience in it. If so, we build in their existing account and merge the eTailPet export into it, keeping their sending history and any existing subscribers.
The flows

Six flows, seventeen emails

Each flow lists its trigger, the emails with send timing, and the logic that keeps it polite — every flow exits on purchase, and global frequency caps prevent overlapping sends.

1

Welcome series

Core
Trigger
New subscriber — popup, footer signup, or checkout opt-in (not the eTailPet import)
Exit
Makes a purchase, or completes the series
KPI
Signup → first-purchase rate
  • ImmediateWelcome + first-order offer. Warm hello, what DogiZone is, single CTA with the welcome code (offer % is a client decision — see Decisions).
  • Day 2More than a store. The Rockville story — retail plus daycare, boarding, grooming, training, DogiWash. Positions DogiZone against Chewy on the thing Chewy can't do: being local.
  • Day 5Bestsellers + proof. Top products by category, Google review pulls, pickup/local-delivery reminder, code expiry nudge.
2

Abandoned cart

Core
Trigger
Cart abandoned — fires via Shopify cart sync, 1 hour after last activity
Exit
Checkout completed
KPI
Recovered revenue (Mailchimp attributes it per-email)
  • +1 hourYou left something. Cart contents with images, one-click return to checkout. No discount — most recoveries don't need one.
  • +24 hoursObjection handling. Free local pickup today, same-day local delivery, staff help line. The local angle is the differentiator — lead with it before discounting.
  • +72 hoursLast nudge. Optional incentive (client decision) or urgency framing on stock. Final email in the flow.
3

Post-purchase

Core
Trigger
First order placed (repeat buyers skip to email 3)
Exit
Completes the series; re-entry allowed per order for email 3
KPI
Review submission rate, second-purchase rate
  • Day 1Thanks — here's how it works. Pickup hours and where to park, delivery window expectations, who to call. Sets up the in-store relationship.
  • Day 10Review request. One product, one click. Coordination note: if Judge.me review requests are enabled in the store build, this email is dropped from the flow — one review ask per order, never two.
  • Day 21Category cross-sell. Bought food → treats and toppers. Bought a leash → collars and ID. Driven by the purchased-category data from the Shopify sync.
4

Replenishment

Core
Trigger
Purchase in a consumable category — dry food, wet food, frozen/raw, treats
Exit
Any new food/treat order
KPI
Repeat-purchase rate, days between orders
  • Day 30*Running low? The exact product they bought, one-click reorder, pickup/delivery choice. *Interval tuned per category — dry food ~35–45 days, wet/frozen ~21, treats ~30.
  • Day 40*Don't run out. Second touch if no reorder — same-day local delivery framing ("out of food is an emergency at dinner time").
This is the money flow for a pet store. Food is a 30–45 day consumable and DogiZone's whole catalog skews consumable. Replenishment is where Mailchimp pays for itself — it's also the natural on-ramp if the client later adds subscriptions in Shopify.
5

Win-back

Core
Trigger
Previous customer, no order in 90 days
Exit
Any purchase
KPI
Reactivation rate
  • Day 90It's been a while. What's new in store, new brands carried, upcoming events (DogiDeals, calendar events).
  • Day 97Come-back offer. Client-approved incentive — discount code or a service tie-in (free DogiWash with $50 order is on-brand and protects product margin).
  • Day 111Last touch. Soft goodbye + one-question feedback ask. Non-responders get suppressed from future sends — keeps the list healthy and deliverability strong.
6

Browse abandonment

Optional · Phase 2
Trigger
Viewed a product, no cart, identified subscriber — 24 hours later
Exit
Adds to cart (hands off to flow 2) or purchases
KPI
View → cart rate
  • +24 hoursStill thinking it over? The viewed product plus related picks. Requires Mailchimp site tracking enabled on the storefront — proposed as phase 2 once the core five flows have a month of data.

Before we build

Five decisions we need

  • Welcome offer — % off, $ off, or a service tie-in (free DogiWash) for first online order
  • Cart incentive — whether email 3 of abandoned cart carries a discount, and how much
  • Win-back offer — the day-97 incentive, sized against product margin
  • Review destination — Judge.me (in the store scope) or Mailchimp email; one, not both
  • Existing Mailchimp account — does DogiZone already have one via eTailPet Plus? Build there or start fresh

Also needed at kickoff: Mailchimp account access (or a green light to create one), and the final eTailPet customer export before the platform is cancelled — eTailPet purges all data at termination.
