Three faults worth seeing in detail

Your best URL is the About page

The address /mini-goldendoodle-puppies-for-sale-california, an exact match for the market you lost, serves your About page. The real California page lives somewhere else. Two URLs, one keyword, and the stronger address points at the wrong content.

The San Diego page went national

Your San Diego page is what currently ranks for "mini goldendoodle breeders", a national term with 4,400 searches a month. Your photo gallery is what ranks for most breed questions. Right demand, wrong pages answering it.

Four pages, one keyword

"English goldendoodle" has been served by four different URLs of yours at different times. Google keeps swapping between them, so none of them holds a position. The same pattern runs through shedding, sizes and generations.

One number we will not put in front of you. SEMrush reports 1,566 lost keywords. Most of those were ranked below position 30 and earned nothing, so quoting that figure would be theatre. The honest recovery list is 137 keywords that held a top-30 position with real volume, worth 51,410 monthly searches between them.

Your three markets

Front-loaded, and honest about what they are worth.

You named San Jose, Seattle and Los Angeles from 18 years of your own buyer records, and they stay first in the build order. We want to be straight about the numbers behind them, because they change what these pages are for.

Market one, San Jose

Bay Area, your strongest market by volume of buyers. The Idaho to California connection, buyers you understand, easy placements.

Market two, Seattle

A different buyer, still a strong fit. Total Washington search demand for doodle terms is 280 a month, across five keywords.

Market three, Los Angeles

Including Long Beach and Orange County. Part of a California picture worth roughly 1,000 on-brand searches a month in total.

These pages convert. They do not bring volume. About 1,300 searches a month exist across all three markets combined, and most of the raw California volume is rescue and adoption intent, which is the opposite of a $3,850 puppy. The volume lives in the national breed terms, which is also where your recoverable rankings sit. So the market pages earn their place by turning the right visitor into a deposit, and the breed pages do the work of finding people.
Where we lean in
  • Fix the California slug conflict, then rebuild the Bay Area, Seattle and SoCal pages
  • Your Seattle page is 148 words today and your San Jose page is 450
  • Lead with the shipping story, since you have no California address and the map pack is closed to you
Where we deliberately do not
  • East Coast, where you said placements fall apart nine times in ten
  • Boise and Idaho, which you do not want to sell into
  • Rescue and adoption searches, and other doodle breeds
A decision only you can make. Your homepage is currently optimised for Boise. The title reads "Mini Goldendoodle Puppies Idaho" and the H1 says "Mini Goldendoodle Puppies for Sale in Boise, Idaho", on the page that carries 70 percent of your traffic. Of roughly 48 geographic pages on the site, about 10 target Idaho and 22 target markets you told us you would rather avoid. Changing that changes what the whole site is about, so we are raising it rather than acting on it.
