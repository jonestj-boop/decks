High-authority earned links

Editorial coverage on the sites that move the needle — pitched to real journalists, written by them, and built to rank you with both Google and the AI engines.

There are two ways to build the authority that lifts you in search and gets you named by the AI engines: you can buy links, or you can earn coverage. This is the earned route — we develop story angles about Master Matchmakers and pitch them to thousands of real journalists. When one decides you're worth writing about, you earn a link on a genuinely authoritative publication. No money changes hands with the publisher, and that's exactly what makes it count.

Earned coverage — this

  • A journalist chooses to write about you
  • No payment to the publisher — a real endorsement
  • DR 70+ publications; the safest profile with Google
  • The version AI assistants are most likely to cite
  • Fully done for you

Paid links — e.g. GetReach

  • You pick a site on a marketplace and pay for the slot
  • A bought placement, not a mention
  • Cheaper and faster — but hands-on, you run it
  • Paid links are Google's gray area
  • Lower trust, and it reads as placement

How it works

  • We create the angles, data points and pitch — you just provide the basic inputs to get started.
  • The angles go out to thousands of real journalists across relevant outlets.
  • Journalists write and publish the coverage; you earn the link.
  • You get a report as each link is secured.

What you get

  • A 10-link campaign typically generates 8–12 links, sometimes more.
  • At least 2 links on DR 70+ sites with a minimum of 1M monthly visitors (SimilarWeb).
  • A natural mix of dofollow and nofollow — a healthy, real link profile.
  • Strong authority signals for Google and the AI engines.

What to know going in

  • These are earned placements, so there's no selecting or vetoing specific sites — the journalists choose what to run. That's what makes the links credible.
  • It's a long game. Don't expect links in the first 30 days; coverage typically starts landing in month 3–4 and builds from there.
  • Larger packages run as 10 outreaches per month until the full quantity is secured (the 100-link package runs across roughly a year), for steady, natural link growth.
