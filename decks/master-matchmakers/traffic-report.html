Executive summary

The headline

Organic traffic is up sharply. Monthly clicks grew from 682 in January to 1,083 in August — a 59% increase — with the breakout starting in June. The last three months each cleared 1,000 clicks after the first five held in the 640–800 range, and nearly four of every five clicks come from people who don't already know the brand.

The signal: the growth is real and recent. June, July, and August are the three strongest months of the window — and July's 1,181 clicks is the single best month, at a 1.13% CTR that's well above the year's baseline.

What's driving it

  • Non-branded discovery — 5,313 of 6,807 clicks (78%) come from non-branded searches like "matchmaker near me" and "matchmaking service." Growth is coming from people finding the brand for the first time, not just fans searching by name.
  • A step-change in June — clicks jumped 34% month over month in June (801 → 1,075) and have stayed above 1,000 since. This is a level shift, not a one-month spike.
  • Click-through is improving — CTR rose from ~0.74% in the first half of the year to 1.0–1.13% in July and August. The site is converting the same visibility into more clicks.

Interpretation

What this means

  • The trajectory is up and holding. This isn't a one-month spike — June, July, and August are three consecutive months above 1,000 clicks, off a first-half baseline in the 640–800 range.
  • Growth is coming from new audiences. 78% of clicks are non-branded. People who've never heard of Master Matchmakers are finding it through category and "near me" searches — the healthiest kind of organic growth.
  • Rankings are the lever, not traffic quality. The biggest category terms and several state pages sit on page two. They already earn impressions; they just need to rank higher to convert them.
  • The state-page network is a repeatable playbook. Where a state page ranks on page one it performs (Virginia, Ohio); where it's stuck on page two or three (Texas) the impressions are waiting. That's a clear, page-by-page growth path.
Bottom line: traffic is up 59% over the window, the gains are recent and durable, and they're driven by non-branded discovery. The next tier of growth is sitting on page two — the category terms and state pages that already earn impressions but haven't yet climbed into click range.
