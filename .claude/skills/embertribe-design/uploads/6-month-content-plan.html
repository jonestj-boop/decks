How to read this plan

Primary Keyword: the main search term each article will be optimized for. Each keyword expands into dozens of related long-tail searches during the writing process.

Monthly Searches: how many Canadians search this exact term each month (SEMrush, Canadian database).

Difficulty (KD): how hard it is to rank on page one of Google. 0–20 Very Easy · 21–35 Easy · 36–50 Moderate · 51–70 Hard.

Pillar: which content cluster the article belongs to. Articles within a pillar link to each other and to a central "hub" post for maximum SEO authority.

Funnel Stage: Top = awareness & education, Middle = comparison & research, Bottom = ready to apply.

REFRESH badge: this article already exists on the site and will be updated and expanded rather than rewritten from scratch — a faster way to recover lost rankings.

Next step: approve this plan and we'll begin writing the Month 1 batch.
