Why this plan

How we picked these 120 keywords — and what we deliberately left out.

Every keyword scored on three things: monthly search volume, keyword difficulty, and brand fit. We picked the 120 that win on all three. The rest we cut on purpose.

Criterion 01

Volume

Real, sustained monthly demand — not vanity terms that never convert.

Criterion 02

Difficulty

Winnable in 6–12 months given your current authority. We front-load the easy wins.

Criterion 03

Brand fit

Topics your buyers actually search on the path to purchasing what you sell.

Deliberately excluded: ultra-high-difficulty head terms with no near-term path to page 1, and high-volume terms with no commercial relevance to your catalog.
