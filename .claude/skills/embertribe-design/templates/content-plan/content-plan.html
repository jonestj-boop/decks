Methodology & how to read this plan

Every article is mapped to a content pillar, a funnel stage (top / middle / bottom), and a search intent (informational, commercial, navigational). Articles are sequenced to front-load low-difficulty, high-relevance wins that can rank quickly, then build toward higher-volume, more competitive terms as domain authority grows.

Monthly searches = combined monthly search volume for the target keyword cluster. Difficulty (KD) = keyword difficulty on a 0–100 scale.
