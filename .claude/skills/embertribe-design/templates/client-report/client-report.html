Findings

What's costing conversions

01

Product page buries the add-to-cart

On mobile the CTA sits below three scrolls of copy. 60% of sessions never reach it. This is the single biggest leak in the funnel.

P1 · CriticalEffort: LowEst. lift: +12%
02

Checkout forces account creation

No guest checkout. Forced sign-up is the #1 cause of abandonment in the category — you're losing an estimated 22% of ready buyers here.

P1 · CriticalEffort: MedEst. lift: +9%
03

No visible trust signals near the buy box

Reviews, returns policy, and payment badges are absent at the decision point. Adding them is a fast, high-confidence win.

P2 · HighEffort: LowEst. lift: +4%

Recommendations

The fix list, in order

1

This week — quick wins

  • Move add-to-cart above the fold on mobile PDP
  • Add review stars + returns badge to the buy box
  • Surface free-shipping threshold in the cart
2

Weeks 2–4 — structural fixes

  • Enable guest checkout
  • Collapse checkout to a single page
  • Add express payment (Apple / Shop Pay)
3

Ongoing — test & learn

  • A/B test PDP layout variants
  • Add exit-intent offer on cart
  • Monthly funnel review against benchmark
